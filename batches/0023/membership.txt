Once upon a time there was a little girl named Lily. She liked to play in the garden with her dog. One sunny day, Lily found a small red ball under a big tree. She picked it up and threw it to her dog. The dog ran fast and brought the ball back. Lily laughed and said, "Good dog!" Then her mother called her for lunch. They ate bread and cheese together at the table. After lunch, Lily and her dog went outside again. They played until the sun went down, and then they both went to sleep.
