## Purpose

The weights of a published model are bytes whose origin is not recorded in
them and is not one process. What constrains them is the evaluator: a finite
dataflow of operators in a finite dtype over a finite input alphabet. For every
tensor, the set of values under which every activation stays finite and every
softmax stays non-degenerate for every input is therefore a definite region, a
system of inequalities over the bytes. No batch has computed any part of it.
This batch computes two parts:

1. **Exact, at length 1.** The function restricted to one-token prompts has
   49,152 inputs. All of them are run. Every next-token distribution, and the
   extremum of every tapped activation over the whole input alphabet, is then
   known exactly, not bounded. This is the complete characterization of the
   model at length 1 in the language of its outputs and its tapped
   activations: nothing about the length-1 restriction is left to sampling.
2. **Sound, at lengths 2 and 8.** With more than one position the input set is
   not enumerable (49,152² at length 2). The batch computes interval bounds
   that contain every activation for every input, with an existing tool,
   auto_LiRPA (interval bound propagation, "IBP"), from the exact coordinate-wise
   hull of the embedding table. The length-1 bounds must contain the length-1
   exact extrema; that is the check that the instrument is sound.

From both, per sort of tensor, the slack: how many doublings a tensor admits
before the bound on what it feeds exceeds the largest finite bf16 value, and
the margin of the attention scores to saturation.

## Sorts of tensor, by use

Read from the model's graph, not from its origin (batch 0028):

| Sort | Tensors | Consumed by |
|---|---|---|
| embedding, tied head | 1 | row lookup on input; inner product with the final normed residual on output |

| q, k projection | 60 | linear, RoPE, bilinear score, softmax |
| v, o projection | 60 | linear, attention-weighted sum, linear, residual add |
| FFN gate | 30 | linear, SiLU |
| FFN up, down | 60 | linear; product with the gated value; linear, residual add |

## Cells

- **01 Exact table at length 1.** Each of the 49,152 token ids as a prompt of
  length 1. Recorded per row: argmax, the sixteen most probable ids and their
  probabilities, entropy, the largest probability, the smallest and largest
  logit, and the SHA-256 of the float32 logit row. Recorded once: the SHA-256
  of the whole 49,152 × 49,152 float32 matrix in id order (the matrix itself,
  9.7 GB, is not kept), the number of distinct argmax ids, the number of
  distinct rows, whether every value is finite. Tapped activations, with the
  exact coordinate-wise minimum and maximum over all 49,152 inputs: the residual
  after the embedding and after each of the 30 blocks, the gate
  pre-activation of each block, the output of the final norm, the logits.
- **02 Sound bounds at lengths 1, 2 and 8.** auto_LiRPA IBP over the model from
  `inputs_embeds`, each position's embedding bounded by the exact coordinate-wise
  minimum and maximum of the embedding table (the hull of the finite input set),
  causal attention, positions 0 to T−1. Bounds of the residual after blocks 1,
  2, 4, 8, 16 and 30 and of the logits.
- **03 Slack.** Per tapped point: log2 of the largest finite bf16 value
  (3.39 × 10³⁸) over the exact or bounded largest magnitude.
- **Controls.** (a) The null variant of batch 0014: one unit in the last place
  of one gain of the last block's post-attention norm; cell 01 repeated. (b) One
  entry of one down-projection set to NaN in a copy; 512 rows of cell 01; the
  finiteness report must say non-finite. (c) Layer 0's down-projection scaled by
  2⁸ in a copy; the IBP upper bound of the residual after block 1 at length 1
  must grow.

## Predictions, with severity

- **P1, moderate.** auto_LiRPA (git commit
  `e3930353a3589f7d10879f1e71223d1eefb57196`) builds the bounded module from
  the `transformers` Llama forward with SiLU written as x·sigmoid(x), RMSNorm
  written with sqrt and division, and eager attention, and computes IBP bounds
  at lengths 1, 2 and 8 without error. Refuted by any error. A refutation names
  the operator it stops at.
- **P2, high.** At length 1, for every tapped point and every coordinate, the
  exact minimum and maximum of cell 01 lie inside the IBP bounds of cell 02.
  Refuted by one coordinate outside. This is the soundness of the instrument;
  if it fails, no bound of this batch is used.
- **P3, moderate.** The exact table is finite everywhere, has 49,152 distinct
  rows, and its argmax takes at least 100 distinct values. Refuted by a
  non-finite value, a duplicate row, or fewer than 100 argmax values. The last
  clause is a rejection condition for a trivial model stated and tested on a
  real one.
- **P4, moderate.** At length 1 the largest residual magnitude over all blocks
  and inputs is below 10³ and the largest logit magnitude below 10². Refuted by
  larger values.
- **P5, moderate.** The IBP bounds stay finite (below the largest bf16 value) at
  lengths 2 and 8 for every tapped point, and at length 8 the width of the
  logit bounds exceeds the exact length-1 range by a factor of at least 10.
  Refuted by an infinite or NaN bound, or by a narrower factor.
- **Control (a), high.** The null variant changes the SHA-256 of the whole
  matrix and changes the argmax in fewer than 0.1% of rows. Refuted otherwise;
  a refutation of the second clause means the argmax at length 1 is not a
  stable readout of the model and P3's third clause is to be read with that.
- **Control (b), high.** The NaN copy is reported non-finite. Refuted if the
  report says finite; then the finiteness report cannot fail and P3's first
  clause is void.
- **Control (c), moderate.** The IBP upper bound of the residual after block 1
  grows by a factor of at least 16 under the 2⁸ scaling. Refuted by a smaller
  factor; then the bounds do not respond to the weights they are claimed to
  bound.

## Observed, no prediction

The exact extremum per tapped point and per block; the distribution of
entropies and of largest probabilities over the 49,152 rows; the slack per
tapped point at each length; the number of coordinates of the embedding table
whose hull width is zero; the time and peak memory of each cell on the runner.

## What is not in this batch

## Addendum 1, registered 2026-10-16 after runs 38973153532 and 38974672638, before cell 04 ran

**What the two runs showed.** The exact table (cell 01) stands. auto_LiRPA's IBP (cell 02) bounds the
residual after block 1 soundly (P2 holds there) but at 9.7 × 10¹⁵ against an exact 42.3, and from block 2 on
the propagated variance interval has a negative lower bound and `sqrt` refuses (P1, P5 refuted). The reason is
arithmetical, not a bug: an interval through x · 1/√(mean(x²) + ε) is widened by 1/√ε = 316 per coordinate
and the widening compounds per block. Generic interval propagation cannot bound this architecture.

**Cell 04, structural bounds.** Two facts of the architecture make bounds length-independent and additive:
(i) the output n of RMSNorm satisfies ‖n‖₂ ≤ √d for every input, so every linear map reading n has
|(W(n ⊙ g))ᵢ| ≤ ‖Wᵢ ⊙ g‖₂ · √d (Cauchy–Schwarz), whatever the residual's magnitude; (ii) softmax weights
are non-negative and sum to one, so an attention output lies in the coordinate-wise hull of the value
vectors, whatever the length and the scores. Per block, from the weights alone: the box of q, k, v, the
attention output (the hull of v), the o-projection by interval arithmetic, the gate pre-activation box, the
SiLU image [−0.2785, max gate], the up box, the down-projection by interval arithmetic. The residual bound
after block k is the embedding hull plus the sum of the first k blocks' attention and FFN boxes. The
logit bound is ‖Eᵢ ⊙ g_f‖₂ · √d. The attention-score bound per head is ‖q‖·‖k‖/√d_h with RoPE norm-preserving.
Every step is a sound over-approximation for every input of every length. The code is numpy over the
weights; no model is run.

- **P6, high.** The structural bounds contain the exact length-1 extrema of cell 01 at every tapped point:
  the residual after every block, every gate pre-activation, the final norm output, the logits. Refuted by
  one coordinate outside. If refuted, the derivation above is wrong and nothing of cell 04 is used.
- **P7, moderate.** The structural bound of the residual after block 30 is below 10⁶ in every coordinate,
  and the logit bound below 10⁴. Refuted by larger values.
- **P8, moderate.** The attention-score magnitude bound is below 10³ for every head of every block.
  Refuted by a larger value.
- **Control (d), moderate.** With layer 0's down-projection scaled by 2⁸ in a copy, block 1's FFN box scales
  by exactly 2⁸ and no other block's box changes. Refuted otherwise.
- **Observed.** Per block, the attention and FFN boxes and their ratio to the exact length-1 extrema (the
  looseness); the slack in doublings per sort against the bf16 ceiling, now for every length; the score
  bounds per head; the time and memory of the cell.

## Results (read from the logs and artifacts of the three runs)

**Cell 01, exact, every one of the 49,152 one-token prompts (144 s, 2.2 GB).**
- Finite everywhere. 49,150 distinct logit rows: ids 5 and 9 (`<file_sep>`, `<issue_comment>`) and ids 7 and 45,175
  (`<gh_stars>`, ` transistorsum`) have identical embedding rows and therefore identical outputs. The input alphabet
  of the model at length 1 has 49,150 distinguishable letters (P3's second clause refuted by the model, its first and
  third clauses held: 1,218 distinct argmax values; token 28 is the argmax of 13,292 inputs).
- Whole-matrix SHA-256 `b7f4c3b035021614d1b1bcb6244bb24e9c8496c149ef528c7195f446e1cac07a` (float32, id order).
- Entropy: min 0.075, median 10.55, max 14.10 bits. Largest probability: min 0.0025, median 0.062, max 0.996.
- The residual stream has one massive coordinate, index 507: 21,119 at block 12, above 20,800 through block 28,
  3,991 after block 30; blocks 3 to 9 reach about 1,050; coordinates 17 and 507 exceed 1,000 after block 30. Gate
  pre-activations reach 50.2 (block 12), 49.3 and 49.5 (blocks 29, 30), below 1.3 in blocks 13 to 27. Logits reach
  41.0. P4 refuted by the model.
- Slack to the largest finite bf16 value: at least 113 doublings at every tapped point.
- Control a: the null variant changes the matrix hash and the argmax of 0 rows. Control b: the NaN copy is reported
  non-finite.

**Cell 02, auto_LiRPA IBP (0.8.0 at the registered commit).** After two faults of the instrument were fixed (the
explicit forward now agrees with `transformers` to 6e-4, 4e-4, 1e-4 at lengths 1, 2, 8; `BoundDiv`'s LayerNorm
branch avoided): the bound of the residual after block 1 is sound (P2 holds there, violation 0) and useless,
9.7 × 10¹⁵ at length 1 against an exact 42.3; from block 2 on the propagated variance has a negative lower bound
and `sqrt` refuses. P1 and P5 refuted. Control c: ratio exactly 256.

**Cell 04, structural bounds from the weights alone, every length (2.6 s).**
- P6 held: all 63 tapped points (31 residual, 30 gate, final norm, logits) contained, maximum violation 0.
- The bounds: residual after block 30 below 1.19 × 10⁷ in every coordinate (P7's threshold of 10⁶ refuted);
  logits below 350.9 against an exact 41.0 (looseness 2.7 at the tightest coordinate, 4.8 median); gate
  pre-activations within 1.9 to 10.8 times the exact extrema; attention scores below 1,880 for every head (P8's
  threshold of 10³ refuted; the largest per-head bounds are in blocks 14 to 19). The residual bounds are loose by
  10³ to 10⁵ because the FFN box sums 1,536 worst cases per block (68,150 for block 1 alone against an exact 42).
- Slack to the largest finite bf16 value, for every input of every length: 104 doublings for the residual, 117 for
  the scores, 120 for the logits. No activation of this model can overflow in float32 or bfloat16 arithmetic for
  any input of any length, and the same holds for every rescaling of the weights by less than 2¹⁰⁴. The
  finiteness inequalities of the feasibility region are therefore not binding anywhere near these weights; what
  binds is precision and softmax behaviour, which this batch does not bound.
- Control d: block 1's FFN box scales by exactly 256, no other block changes.

**What the batch establishes about completeness.** The restriction of the model to length 1 is characterised
completely in the language of its outputs and tapped activations: every value is computed, hashed and stored or
summarised. For every other length, every activation has a sound finite bound from the weights alone, and the
bounds are tight within a factor of 3 to 5 at the logits and loose by three to five orders of magnitude on the
residual stream. The derivation is two lines of mathematics about the architecture and could be stated as a
theorem over the open term; batches 0020 to 0022's Lean definitions are the place for that.
