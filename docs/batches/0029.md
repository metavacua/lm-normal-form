## Purpose

The weights of a published model are bytes whose origin is not recorded in
them and is not one process. What constrains them is the evaluator: a finite
dataflow of operators in a finite dtype over a finite input alphabet. For every
tensor, the set of values under which every activation stays finite and every
softmax stays non-degenerate for every input is therefore a definite region, a
system of inequalities over the bytes. No batch has computed any part of it.
This batch computes two parts:

1. **Exact, at length 1.** The function restricted to one-token prompts has
   49,152 inputs. All of them are run. Every next-token distribution, and the
   extremum of every tapped activation over the whole input alphabet, is then
   known exactly, not bounded. This is the complete characterization of the
   model at length 1 in the language of its outputs and its tapped
   activations: nothing about the length-1 restriction is left to sampling.
2. **Sound, at lengths 2 and 8.** With more than one position the input set is
   not enumerable (49,152² at length 2). The batch computes interval bounds
   that contain every activation for every input, with an existing tool,
   auto_LiRPA (interval bound propagation, "IBP"), from the exact coordinate-wise
   hull of the embedding table. The length-1 bounds must contain the length-1
   exact extrema; that is the check that the instrument is sound.

From both, per sort of tensor, the slack: how many doublings a tensor admits
before the bound on what it feeds exceeds the largest finite bf16 value, and
the margin of the attention scores to saturation.

## Sorts of tensor, by use

Read from the model's graph, not from its origin (batch 0028):

| Sort | Tensors | Consumed by |
|---|---|---|
| embedding, tied head | 1 | row lookup on input; inner product with the final normed residual on output |
| RMSNorm gain | 61 | per-coordinate multiply after normalization |
| q, k projection | 60 | linear, RoPE, bilinear score, softmax |
| v, o projection | 60 | linear, attention-weighted sum, linear, residual add |

| FFN up, down | 60 | linear; product with the gated value; linear, residual add |

## Cells

- **01 Exact table at length 1.** Each of the 49,152 token ids as a prompt of
  length 1. Recorded per row: argmax, the sixteen most probable ids and their
  probabilities, entropy, the largest probability, the smallest and largest
  logit, and the SHA-256 of the float32 logit row. Recorded once: the SHA-256
  of the whole 49,152 × 49,152 float32 matrix in id order (the matrix itself,
  9.7 GB, is not kept), the number of distinct argmax ids, the number of
  distinct rows, whether every value is finite. Tapped activations, with the
  exact coordinate-wise minimum and maximum over all 49,152 inputs: the residual
  after the embedding and after each of the 30 blocks, the gate
  pre-activation of each block, the output of the final norm, the logits.
- **02 Sound bounds at lengths 1, 2 and 8.** auto_LiRPA IBP over the model from
  `inputs_embeds`, each position's embedding bounded by the exact coordinate-wise
  minimum and maximum of the embedding table (the hull of the finite input set),
  causal attention, positions 0 to T−1. Bounds of the residual after blocks 1,
  2, 4, 8, 16 and 30 and of the logits.
- **03 Slack.** Per tapped point: log2 of the largest finite bf16 value
  (3.39 × 10³⁸) over the exact or bounded largest magnitude.
- **Controls.** (a) The null variant of batch 0014: one unit in the last place
  of one gain of the last block's post-attention norm; cell 01 repeated. (b) One
  entry of one down-projection set to NaN in a copy; 512 rows of cell 01; the
  finiteness report must say non-finite. (c) Layer 0's down-projection scaled by
  2⁸ in a copy; the IBP upper bound of the residual after block 1 at length 1
  must grow.

## Predictions, with severity

- **P1, moderate.** auto_LiRPA (git commit
  `e3930353a3589f7d10879f1e71223d1eefb57196`) builds the bounded module from
  the `transformers` Llama forward with SiLU written as x·sigmoid(x), RMSNorm
  written with sqrt and division, and eager attention, and computes IBP bounds
  at lengths 1, 2 and 8 without error. Refuted by any error. A refutation names
  the operator it stops at.
- **P2, high.** At length 1, for every tapped point and every coordinate, the
  exact minimum and maximum of cell 01 lie inside the IBP bounds of cell 02.
  Refuted by one coordinate outside. This is the soundness of the instrument;
  if it fails, no bound of this batch is used.
- **P3, moderate.** The exact table is finite everywhere, has 49,152 distinct
  rows, and its argmax takes at least 100 distinct values. Refuted by a
  non-finite value, a duplicate row, or fewer than 100 argmax values. The last
  clause is a rejection condition for a trivial model stated and tested on a
  real one.
- **P4, moderate.** At length 1 the largest residual magnitude over all blocks
  and inputs is below 10³ and the largest logit magnitude below 10². Refuted by
  larger values.
- **P5, moderate.** The IBP bounds stay finite (below the largest bf16 value) at
  lengths 2 and 8 for every tapped point, and at length 8 the width of the
  logit bounds exceeds the exact length-1 range by a factor of at least 10.
  Refuted by an infinite or NaN bound, or by a narrower factor.
- **Control (a), high.** The null variant changes the SHA-256 of the whole
  matrix and changes the argmax in fewer than 0.1% of rows. Refuted otherwise;
  a refutation of the second clause means the argmax at length 1 is not a
  stable readout of the model and P3's third clause is to be read with that.
- **Control (b), high.** The NaN copy is reported non-finite. Refuted if the
  report says finite; then the finiteness report cannot fail and P3's first
  clause is void.
- **Control (c), moderate.** The IBP upper bound of the residual after block 1
  grows by a factor of at least 16 under the 2⁸ scaling. Refuted by a smaller
  factor; then the bounds do not respond to the weights they are claimed to
  bound.

## Observed, no prediction

The exact extremum per tapped point and per block; the distribution of
entropies and of largest probabilities over the 49,152 rows; the slack per
tapped point at each length; the number of coordinates of the embedding table
whose hull width is zero; the time and peak memory of each cell on the runner.

## What is not in this batch

Lengths above 8, any input distribution (every statement is over the whole
finite alphabet or a sound superset of it), any quantized carrier, and any
statement about the origin of the weights.

## Resources

One runner, 7 GB. The model in float32 is 540 MB. Cell 01 runs 96 batches of
512 one-token prompts; the logits of a batch are 100 MB. Cell 02 holds the
model and two bound tensors per node; at length 8 this is small. Expected
time: under 30 minutes per cell.
