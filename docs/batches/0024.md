# Batch 0024: how much the noise of a quantized run varies

- **Registered:** 2026-10-07, before the first run (the commit that adds this file and `batches/0024/grade24.py`, which holds every claim and every threshold, precedes the commit that triggers the run). The thresholds of N1 and N2 were set after the results of batch 0014's addendum 3 had been read.
- **Kind:** an experiment batch: the control that batch 0014 left unfinished.
- **Status:** run once (2026-10-07, CI run 37621928683, commit a9f99b4) and graded from the committed files by `grade24.py`: **22 claims, 21 as predicted (N1 8 of 8, N2 7 of 8, N3 6 of 6), 1 REFUTED (N2 for CTranslate2); 32 of 32 controls hold.** Results below.
- **Branch:** `batch/0024-null-spread`, on top of batch 0014. **Workflow:** `.github/workflows/batch-0024.yml` (run by a commit whose message contains the bracketed word run). **Code:** `batches/0014/nulls.py` (the null variants; new), `batches/0014/variants.py` and `batches/0014/xrt.py` (changed: the nulls are made by `nulls.py`; `xrt.py` can delete a variant's converted files after it has been run, `LMNF_CLEAN=1`), `batches/0024/grade24.py`, `batches/0024/test_nulls.py`.
  **Depends on:** batch 0014 (the variants, the driver, the llama.cpp and CTranslate2 adapters, P17).

No LARQL code, text or data is used in this batch.

## Purpose

Quantizing a model's weights, or its cache, to blocks of few bits makes the output depend on how the numbers fall on the grid. A transformation of the weights that is exact in real arithmetic (a permutation of units, a sign, a power of two) can then change the output of a quantized run by a little, because the numbers fall differently. How little is "a little"? Batch 0014 answered with a **null variant**: the same checkpoint with every norm weight multiplied by 1 + 2⁻²³, one unit in the last place of float32, which is not a symmetry and changes the arithmetic by the least that float32 can express. The error of a variant against its original in the same cell, in units of the null's, is then the measure; its addendum 3 found, in seven cells on one machine, that the variants that the derivation says commute with the quantizer are at most 1.85 times the null and those that it says do not are at least 2.35 times, the narrowest ratio between the two classes being 2.5 (Q8_0 weights).

That rests on **one** null per cell. Batch 0014 did not measure the spread of the null itself, and recorded the variants claimed the same at 0.4 to 1.5 times the null across processors (a factor 3.75), which is more than the narrowest gap (2.5). This batch measures the spread: **eight** nulls per cell, in the same job as the variants and the original, on one machine, and grades the classes of 0014 against them. It adds an eighth cell, CTranslate2 in int8, which batch 0014 graded (P7) with the ratio rule of e_q and without a null.

## Subjects

Everything here is about **SmolLM2-135M-Instruct** (revision `12fd25f77366fa6b3b4b768ec3050bf629380bac`), a language model by the registered test of batch 0023 (docs/theory/special-theory.md §0); the test is not repeated here. The runtimes are llama.cpp b11450 (the prebuilt release, checked against its checksum, and its source at the commit `a46709b683aba9274d8ab29f5b42f7e551d1dff6`) and CTranslate2 4.8.2.

## The null variants

Defined in `batches/0014/nulls.py`, tested by `batches/0024/test_nulls.py` (it runs first in CI and stops the job on failure):

| name | what changes |
|---|---|
| `nullall` | every norm weight times 1 + 2⁻²³ (the null of batch 0014) |
| `nullm` | every norm weight times 1 − 2⁻²⁴ |
| `nullr1` … `nullr6` | every norm weight times 1 + s 2⁻²³, the sign s of each element drawn from a generator seeded with 1000 + k |

Each is computed in float64 and rounded to float32; only the norm weights change (the test checks this on a synthetic checkpoint), by at most two units in the last place, and nearly all of them change. They were defined before any of them was run: this is not the situation of addendum 2 of batch 0014, whose null was defined after the heavy run's results had been read.

## The cells and the classes

Eight cells, each one job: llama.cpp with Q8_0 weights; Q4_0 weights; a cache in f16; in Q8_0 and Q4_0 with its Hadamard rotation and without (`-norot`); and CTranslate2 in int8. In each: the original, the nine exact variants (`scale`, `perm`, `all`, `canon`, `heads`, `units`, `units_blk`, `resid`, `resid_blk`), the control `broken` (the permutation of the hidden coordinates applied to the layers and not to the embedding) and the eight nulls. The classes are those that batch 0014 registered before its runs (docs/batches/0014.md, addendum 3):

- weights cells: *same* = `heads`, `units_blk`, `resid_blk`; *different* = `units`, `resid`, `scale`, `perm`, `all`, `canon` (they do not preserve the 32-element blocks of the quantizer);
- cache cells: *same* = `heads`, `perm`, `resid`, `resid_blk`, `units`, `units_blk`; *different* = `scale`, `all`, `canon`;
- the f16 cache: all nine *same*;
- **CTranslate2 int8: all nine *same*** (registered here). CTranslate2 quantizes the weights of each layer per row, with a scale from the row's largest absolute value, and the activations with a dynamic per-row scale; the registration argued that a permutation of the columns, a change of sign and a multiplication of a row by a power of two leave the quantized integers unchanged up to the same permutation, sign and exponent. This contradicts the classes that batch 0014 registered for this cell in P7: `heads`, `units`, `resid`, `perm` the same; `scale`, `all`, `canon`, which scale columns, different (batch 0014 graded P7 as passed).

## Claims

Three kinds, per cell (22 claims; `docs/batches/0024/claims.md` lists them with the exact comparison of each). The NMSE of a variant is that of its logits against the original's in the same runtime and cell (`vs_same_runtime_original/nmse` of the driver's summary).

| id | claim | where the threshold comes from |
|---|---|---|
| N1 (8 cells, high) | the largest of the eight nulls is at most 3 times the smallest, and the smallest is above zero | chosen, not derived; batch 0014 recorded the same class at 0.4 to 1.5 times the null across processors (a different set of measurements, seen before this registration) |
| N2 (8, high) | every variant claimed the same is at most 4 times the **median** of the eight nulls | the 4 of P17 (a), chosen in addendum 3 of batch 0014 after the earlier data had been read; the single null of P17 is replaced by the median of eight |
| N3 (6, high) | every variant claimed different is at least 1.5 times the **largest** of the eight nulls | chosen, not derived; asks whether the different class is separated from the whole null distribution and not from one draw of it |

**What I expect.** N2 in the cells where batch 0014 found a wide gap; N1 uncertain (the spread of eight draws of this kind of noise is not known to me); N3 in the Q8_0-weights cell is close to a coin flip (the smallest different variant there, `units`, was 2.35 times one null; the largest of eight is probably above 1.5 times that null), and N2 for CTranslate2 int8 depends on the class registered above. A refutation of any is a result: if N1 fails, the null is not a single number and the classification of batch 0014 rested on one draw; if N3 fails in the Q8_0-weights cell, `units` and `resid` cannot be told from the noise there; the claim of batch 0014 that these transformations do not commute with that quantizer is then not established by measurement in that cell, whatever the derivation says.

**Controls** (listed apart, never counted among the claims): the original run twice gives the same generations, the same logits bit for bit and the same perplexity (each cell); the control `broken` is at least 10 times the largest null (each cell). `grade24.py --selftest`, the first thing CI runs, builds a fixture that satisfies every claim and control, violates each claim's own inputs one at a time (REFUTED), removes the data ("not run"), and shows that a NaN refutes: 22 claims and 32 controls pass it.

**What this says, in plain terms.**

1. *The spread of the null, and what is new.* Eight different one-to-two-unit-in-the-last-place changes of the norm weights, in the same job as the variants, give a noise whose largest value is 1.25 to 1.60 times its smallest, in all eight cells (N1: at most 3 was predicted). Batch 0014 had recorded the same class at 0.4 to 1.5 times the null across processors; on one machine the spread of the null is within a factor 1.6 in these cells. In six of the seven llama.cpp cells the job ran on the same processor model as batch 0014's job `nullsame` (AMD EPYC 7763), and the NMSE of all ten variants (the nine exact variants and `broken`) is identical to batch 0014's in each of those six (the seventh, Q8_0 cache not rotated, ran on an Intel Xeon Platinum 8573C: none of the ten is identical, and they differ by up to 29%). So in six cells the variant side of the comparisons, including the ratio 2.5 of the Q8_0-weights cell, is batch 0014's numbers recomputed; the new data are the eight nulls in every cell, and the variants of the Q8_0-cache-not-rotated and CTranslate2 cells.
2. *The classes of batch 0014 against the whole null distribution and not one draw of it.* In the six llama.cpp cells that have a class claimed different, the exact variants that preserve the 32-element blocks of the quantizer (cache: the permutations of units, heads and hidden coordinates; weights: `heads`, `units_blk`, `resid_blk`) are at most 1.6 times the median null and, in the Q8_0 cells, below the largest null; the variants that do not preserve the blocks are at least 2.2 times the largest null in the narrowest cell (Q8_0 weights, `units`), 4 to 6 times in the Q8_0 cache cells and 22 to 49 times in the Q4_0 cells (N2 and N3 as predicted in all of them). In the f16-cache cell all nine variants are claimed the same and are at most 1.18 times the median null. The margin in the Q8_0-weights cell is not large: `units` is 2.2 times the largest of eight nulls against a registered 1.5, and 49 times with Q4_0 weights.
3. *CTranslate2 int8: N2 as registered is refuted, and the classes of batch 0014's P7 hold* (83 times the median null where at most 4 was claimed; the registration put all nine variants in the same class, against P7). The variants that rescale columns of a matrix (`scale`, `all`, `canon`, `units_blk`) are 4 to 83 times the median null (P7's different class: `scale`, `all`, `canon`); the pure permutations and sign changes (`perm`, `resid`, `resid_blk`) are at the level of the null (0.7 to 0.8 times the median) and `heads` and `units` are exactly zero (P7's same class: `heads`, `units`, `resid`, `perm`). CTranslate2 gives every *row* of a weight matrix its own scale, so a rescaling of a *column* changes the largest value of the rows and so integers in them; this is the rule in Claim 1 of batch 0014 ("scalings are compatible only on rows"). The refutation is of this batch's registration; the claim was registered to be refutable and it was.
4. *Controls.* Every cell: the original run twice gives the same generations, logits (bit for bit) and perplexity; the broken variant is 400 (CTranslate2) to 18 million (f16 cache) times the largest null.

**A post hoc reading of the CTranslate2 cell (made after seeing the data; registered nowhere).** The nine exact variants fall in three groups: exactly zero (`heads`, `units`); at the level of the nulls (`perm`, `resid`, `resid_blk`: 0.67 to 0.79 times the median null, i.e. below the largest null); and far above it (`canon` 4.3, `all` 62, `units_blk` 66, `scale` 83 times the median null). With the classes of P7 of batch 0014 and the two variants that P7 did not name placed by the same rule (`resid_blk`, a permutation: same; `units_blk`, which scales columns: different), "same" = {`heads`, `units`, `perm`, `resid`, `resid_blk`} and "different" = {`canon`, `units_blk`, `all`, `scale`}; N2 would hold (largest same 0.79 of the median null) and N3 would hold (smallest different, `canon`, 3.8 times the largest null). That is a statement about this one cell and one model.

**Also visible in the raw files (not claimed).** Greedy generation is chaotic under this noise: a null variant leaves 6 to 10 of the 16 generated texts unchanged in the Q8_0-weights cell (median 8), 5 to 9 in Q4_0, 2 to 5 in CTranslate2 and 3 to 5 with a Q4_0 cache; with an f16 cache all 16 are unchanged for every null. A transformation that the derivation says is exact therefore cannot be judged by whether the generated text is the same: it is judged against the nulls.

## Known weaknesses

- One model, one llama.cpp build, one pool of machines of one kind per job; nobody else has re-run it. The eight cells run on whatever processor each job gets: within a cell the original, the variants and the nulls are on one machine, and that is what the claims compare; across cells they are not comparable, and no claim compares them.
- The null is one family of perturbations (one unit in the last place of the norm weights). The noise of a run also comes from the order of its sums (threads), the kernels, and the rounding of the activations; none of those is perturbed here, so "at most 3 times the spread of the null" is a statement about this family.
- Eight draws are a small sample of a distribution; N1 is a claim about the ratio of the extremes of eight.
- The classes of the llama.cpp cells are those of batch 0014 and come from the block structure of the quantizer. The thresholds of N1 (3), N2 (4) and N3 (1.5) were chosen, not derived, and those of N1 and N2 after batch 0014's addendum 3 had been read.
- In six of the seven llama.cpp cells the variants' NMSE are the numbers of batch 0014's job `nullsame` (same processor model, deterministic); only the nulls are new there.
- The processors differ between cells (AMD EPYC 7763 in six, Intel Xeon Platinum 8573C in two): all comparisons are within a cell, and the cells are not comparable with each other.
- One checkpoint (SmolLM2-135M-Instruct) and one llama.cpp build: the spread of the null and the margins are properties of this model's activations and of these kernels.
- `broken` and the determinism are controls; they show the harness can see a large difference and is reproducible; they do not show that the nulls are the right yardstick.
