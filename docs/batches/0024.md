## Purpose

Quantizing a model's weights, or its cache, to blocks of few bits makes the output depend on how the numbers fall on the grid. A transformation of the weights that is exact in real arithmetic (a permutation of units, a sign, a power of two) can then change the output of a quantized run by a little, because the numbers fall differently. How little is "a little"? Batch 0014 answered with a **null variant**: the same checkpoint with every norm weight multiplied by 1 + 2⁻²³, one unit in the last place of float32, which is not a symmetry and changes the arithmetic by the least that float32 can express. The error of a variant against its original in the same cell, in units of the null's, is then the measure; its addendum 3 found, in seven cells on one machine, that the variants that the derivation says commute with the quantizer are at most 1.85 times the null and those that it says do not are at least 2.35 times, the narrowest ratio between the two classes being 2.5 (Q8_0 weights).

That rests on **one** null per cell. The document of batch 0014 says so ("the spread of the null itself, from several perturbations of the norm weights, was not measured") and records that two realizations of the same class span a factor of about three (0.41 to 0.92, on other processors), which is more than the narrowest gap. A separation of 2.5 against a noise whose own spread is of that size is not established by one realization. This batch measures the spread: **eight** nulls per cell, in the same job as the variants and the original, on one machine, and grades the claims of 0014 again against them. It adds a ninth runtime cell, CTranslate2 in int8, which batch 0014 graded with the rule that cannot fail (below) and without a null.

## The null variants

Defined in `batches/0014/nulls.py`, tested by `batches/0024/test_nulls.py` (it runs first in CI and stops the job on failure):

| name | what changes |
|---|---|
| `nullall` | every norm weight times 1 + 2⁻²³ (the null of batch 0014) |
| `nullm` | every norm weight times 1 − 2⁻²⁴ |
| `nullr1` … `nullr6` | every norm weight times 1 + s 2⁻²³, the sign s of each element drawn from a generator seeded with 1000 + k |

Each is computed in float64 and rounded to float32; only the norm weights change (the test checks this on a synthetic checkpoint), by at most two units in the last place, and nearly all of them change. They were defined before any of them was run: this is not the situation of addendum 2 of batch 0014, whose null was defined after the heavy run's results had been read.

## The cells and the classes

Eight cells, each one job: llama.cpp with Q8_0 weights; Q4_0 weights; a cache in f16; in Q8_0 and Q4_0 with its Hadamard rotation and without (`-norot`); and CTranslate2 in int8. In each: the original, the nine exact variants (`scale`, `perm`, `all`, `canon`, `heads`, `units`, `units_blk`, `resid`, `resid_blk`), the control `broken` (the permutation of the hidden coordinates applied to the layers and not to the embedding) and the eight nulls. The classes are those that batch 0014 registered before its runs (docs/batches/0014.md, addendum 3):

- weights cells: *same* = `heads`, `units_blk`, `resid_blk`; *different* = `units`, `resid`, `scale`, `perm`, `all`, `canon` (they do not preserve the 32-element blocks of the quantizer);
- cache cells: *same* = `heads`, `perm`, `resid`, `resid_blk`, `units`, `units_blk`; *different* = `scale`, `all`, `canon`;
- the f16 cache: all nine *same*;
- **CTranslate2 int8: all nine *same*. This class is new and is derived by hand here, not registered earlier**: CTranslate2 quantizes the weights of each layer per row, with a scale from the row's largest absolute value, and the activations with a dynamic per-row scale; a permutation of the columns, a change of sign and a multiplication of a row by a power of two leave the quantized integers unchanged up to the same permutation, sign and exponent; if that is wrong N2 for this cell is refuted.

## Claims

Four kinds, per cell (28 claims; `docs/batches/0024/claims.md` lists them with the exact comparison of each). The NMSE of a variant is that of its logits against the original's in the same runtime and cell (`vs_same_runtime_original/nmse` of the driver's summary).

| id | claim | where the threshold comes from |
|---|---|---|
| N1 (8 cells, high) | the largest of the eight nulls is at most 3 times the smallest, and the smallest is above zero | the factor of about three between realizations of one class that batch 0014 recorded (a different set of measurements, seen before this registration) |
| N2 (8, high) | every variant claimed the same is at most 4 times the **median** of the eight nulls | the 4 of P17 (a), registered in addendum 3 of batch 0014 after addendum 2's data had been read; the single null of P17 is replaced by the median of eight |
| N3 (6, high) | every variant claimed different is at least 1.5 times the **largest** of the eight nulls | new; chosen to ask whether the different class is separated from the whole null distribution and not from one draw of it |
| N4 (6, high) | every variant claimed different is above every variant claimed the same | P17 (b) of batch 0014, registered before its run (addendum 3), now on a new job |

**What I expect.** N4 and N2 in the cells where batch 0014 found a wide gap; N1 uncertain (the spread of eight draws of this kind of noise is not known to me); N3 in the Q8_0-weights cell is close to a coin flip (the smallest different variant there, `units`, was 2.35 times one null; the largest of eight is probably above 1.5 times that null), and N2 for CTranslate2 int8 depends on the class derived above. A refutation of any is a result: if N1 fails, the null is not a single number and the classification of batch 0014 rested on one draw; if N3 fails in the Q8_0-weights cell, `units` and `resid` cannot be told from the noise there; the claim of batch 0014 that these transformations do not commute with that quantizer is then not established by measurement in that cell, whatever the derivation says.

**Controls** (listed apart, never counted among the claims): the original run twice gives the same generations, the same logits bit for bit and the same perplexity (each cell); the control `broken` is at least 10 times the largest null (each cell). `grade24.py --selftest`, the first thing CI runs, builds a fixture that satisfies every claim and control, violates each claim's own inputs one at a time (REFUTED), removes the data ("not run"), and shows that a NaN refutes: 28 claims and 32 controls pass it. (Its first run found a fault of mine: the functions that compare groups of numbers closed over loop variables and used the sizes of the last cell for every cell; fixed before registration.)

## What was seen before this registration, and what was not

Nothing of this batch has been run. `test_nulls.py` ran locally on a synthetic checkpoint (numpy only). The numbers quoted in the Purpose are those of batch 0014's addendum 3 and its results table (`docs/batches/0014/results/graded_final.md`), and they influenced the thresholds of N1 and N2 as stated above. The audit of 2026-10-07 (another session's) found that batch 0014's null was defined after the heavy run's data, that P16's thresholds were those of the observed ratios, and that rule R1 of 0014 cannot fail; I confirmed the last by reading `grade14.py` (the three NMSEs against one reference make `d ≤ 4·max(e_orig, e_variant)` a consequence of the triangle inequality; 24 lines of the final table are decided by it; 174 are controls; 13 (P16, P17) were registered after data: 161 lines of 372 tested something registered before the data and could fail). None of that table's "372 of 384" is used here.

## Known weaknesses

- One model, one llama.cpp build, one pool of machines of one kind per job; nobody else has re-run it. The eight cells run on whatever processor each job gets: within a cell the original, the variants and the nulls are on one machine, and that is what the claims compare; across cells they are not comparable, and no claim compares them.
- The null is one family of perturbations (one unit in the last place of the norm weights). The noise of a run also comes from the order of its sums (threads), the kernels, and the rounding of the activations; none of those is perturbed here, so "at most 3 times the spread of the null" is a statement about this family.
- Eight draws are a small sample of a distribution; N1 is a claim about the ratio of the extremes of eight.
- The classes of the llama.cpp cells are those of batch 0014 and come from the block structure of the quantizer; that of CTranslate2 is derived here.
- `broken` and the determinism are controls; they show the harness can see a large difference and is reproducible; they do not show that the nulls are the right yardstick.
