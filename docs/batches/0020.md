## Purpose

Batch 0015 tested the symmetries of a Llama-style Transformer by randomized identity testing over a finite field with random oracles in the place of exp, SiLU and the norm function, and batch 0019 computed by a
Datalog program, from the reified architecture, which group of changes of basis acts on each index space, taking as given a library: a lattice of groups, and the group that each *role* of an operation allows. The
library is mathematics assumed. A proof assistant can check it, and "for every nonlinearity" is what a theorem with a universally quantified function says without a probability bound.

Each theorem below justifies an entry of `compat` in `states.dl` (all but `rmsnorm0`, see below), over an arbitrary field `K` (so for the reals and for the finite fields of batch 0015 alike) with the nonlinearity a variable:

| role (group) of the library | theorem | statement |
|---|---|---|
| `rmsnorm` (`O`) | `rms_equivariant`, `preservesDot_of_transpose_mul` | for every function `s`, `rms s (Q x) = Q (rms s x)` when `Q` preserves the dot product, in particular when `Qᵀ Q = 1` |
| `lin` (`GL`) | `ov_gauge`, `mulVec_weighted_sum` | the value/output gauge: `(Wo B)(Σ wᵢ (A Wv) xᵢ) = Wo (Σ wᵢ Wv xᵢ)` when `B A = 1`, for any attention weights |
| `bilinear` (`GL`) | `bilinear_gauge` | `(Bᵀ q) · (A k) = q · k` when `B A = 1` |
| `rope` (`Cplx`) | `cplx_comm`, `cplx_transpose`, `cplx_inv_mul`, `rope_score_gauge`, `rope_gauge_complex` | complex scalars `[[a, -b], [b, a]]` commute with the rotations of a plane, so a complex scalar on the keys and its inverse transpose on the queries leave every rotated score unchanged; the same in `ℂ` |
| `replica` (`Perm`) | `head_perm` | a permutation of the heads or groups does not change a sum over them |
| `mono` (`Mono`) | `swiglu_scale` | scaling the up rows by `c i ≠ 0` and the down columns by `(c i)⁻¹` leaves the block unchanged for every gate function `f` |
| `nl` (`Perm`) | `unit_perm` | permuting the units and the columns of the down matrix leaves the block unchanged |
| the composition | `stack_equivariant`, `residual_equivariant`, `model_invariant` | if a change of variables carries every block, the embedding, and the readout does not see it, the models compute the same function |

## What this does not do

- It does not prove that the groups are the *largest* ones (the completeness of the list of symmetries): the theorems are the soundness direction, one entry at a time. The dimension count of batch 0015 and the derivation of batch 0019 are the evidence for completeness.
- It does not prove the equivariance of a whole block: the glue from the operation-level lemmas to the block-level hypothesis of `model_invariant` (the definitions of attention with a causal mask and of the gated block in Lean, and the proof that each is equivariant by the lemmas) is not here. `model_invariant` is the last step of the argument, not the argument.
- It does not prove the library entry `rmsnorm0` (`CO`: a positive scalar times an orthogonal matrix, for the root mean square with epsilon zero). That entry, and the `+1` of the dimension formula for epsilon zero, rest on the measurements of batch 0015 (the rank deficiency with epsilon zero is one more than with a positive epsilon, in the configurations tested), not on a theorem.
- The correspondence between the Lean definitions and `model.py` is by reading; the roles in `ir.py` are assigned by me.
- The soft spots of the library that the theorems do not touch: the lattice (a finite combinatorial fact that Souffle checks), the dimension of each group (arithmetic).

**What this establishes.** For every field `K` and every function in the place of the normalization's scale, the gate's nonlinearity and the attention's exponential: the root-mean-square normalization commutes with every matrix that preserves the dot product; the value/output gauge leaves the attention output unchanged for any attention weights; a complex scalar on the keys and its inverse transpose on the queries leave every rotated score unchanged (and the same statement in the complex plane); a permutation of heads or of units, and the scaling of the up branch of the gated block, leave the block unchanged; a stack of blocks that a change of variables carries to another stack, with an embedding that it carries and a readout that does not see it, computes the same function. These are the entries of the library of the Datalog program of batch 0019, except `rmsnorm0`, proved in the soundness direction, one entry at a time.

**What it does not.** The gluing of these facts into the statement that a whole attention block and a whole gated block (with a causal mask, a softmax, several heads sharing a key/value group) are equivariant, and hence that the logits of the model are invariant, is not in this batch: `model_invariant` is the last step of that argument, and the premises of its hypotheses are what batch 0021 proves from the definitions. That the groups of the library are the largest ones is not proved either. The `rmsnorm0` entry is not proved (see "What this does not do").
