## Purpose

A pasted LLM-written overview ("When you rigorously formulate a Transformer as an algebraic operator ...") makes four groups of claims about Transformers as algebraic objects: permutation
equivariance and "weight encryption" by a secret permutation; SVD, LoRA and spectral analysis of attention circuits; the Cayley-Hamilton theorem as a path to layer pruning; group theory,
invariant subspaces and basis change. The request was to formalize it programmatically, decompose it into testable and provable claims that yield executable programs with language-model runtime
behaviour as their demonstration, and to develop what survives. This batch takes the first group and the symmetry content of the fourth: every statement about which transformations of the weights leave
the function unchanged, and what follows for obfuscating a model. The second group is batch 0016's, the third batch 0017's; the survivors of all of them are developed in batch 0018.

Six literature searches were made first (the notes are in the session's research folder; the sources are cited where they are used below). What they changed in the plan:

- *The statements are mostly published; the exact certification is not found.* The symmetry facts (no privileged residual basis, the orthogonal gauge with folded norms, the GL(d_head) gauge of attention
  and its restriction to complex scalars under RoPE, conditional linearity given frozen gates) are in Elhage et al. 2021, SliceGPT, QuaRot, Tran et al. (ICLR 2025), Wang and Wang, Golden (2025). Random testing
  over finite fields to decide equivalence of tensor programs is standard (PET, TASO, Mirage at OSDI 2025, Gulwani and Necula's random interpretation). I found no earlier use of an exact F_p forward pass of
  a Llama-style model to certify weight-space symmetries; an absence in searches is not a proof of absence.
- *"For every nonlinearity" needs random oracles, not a polynomial.* My first version of the formal tier used polynomial surrogates for exp, SiLU and the norm function and said that a pass proved the structure for every
  nonlinearity. It proves it for those polynomials. The version below has both, and says which claim each proves.
- *The pasted attribution of the Cayley-Hamilton claim is wrong* (arXiv 2407.00958 is a universal-approximation paper whose pruning remark is a triangle inequality); batch 0017 takes that up.

## The pasted statements, audited

Verdicts are from the claims below and the literature; "T..", "H..", "A..", "M..", "N..", "C.." are claims of `claims.py`, "D.." are real-model cells.

| pasted statement | verdict | corrected statement | tested by |
|---|---|---|---|
| "a vanilla Transformer encoder is fundamentally permutation equivariant" | true only without positional terms and masks | a stack of blocks whose only cross-token operation is unmasked attention, with nothing that refers to a position, satisfies f(x o pi) = f(x) o pi; models with learned or rotary positions, ALiBi or a causal mask do not; no causal decoder is equivariant except degenerately | T1, T2, T3, T4; D1 (BERT-tiny with and without its positions; SmolLM2) |
| (the same) "shuffle the input token rows" | depends on where the positions are attached | relabeling covariance: permute tokens, positions and mask together | T1 to T4 |
| a causal decoder keeps some invariance | partly | with no positional information and ONE layer, the last position is invariant to permutations of the earlier tokens; with two layers it is not | T5, T6; D1 |
| "column permutations ... do not commute unless you apply the exact same transformation to the weight matrices" | the conclusion holds, "exact same" does not | each tensor is transformed on its stream-indexed axes only, readers on the right (columns), writers on the left (rows) | H1, H1t |
| "W_new = P_C W P_C^T" | correct only for square matrices that read and write the stream; wrong for the actual weights | as above; applied to the square weights alone it breaks the model | H2; D2 |
| "applying a secret permutation to an LLM's weights, the model becomes entirely broken and produces gibberish" | false as worded | a consistent permutation of all weights (embedding and head included) leaves the function exactly unchanged: nothing to hide; only a permutation of the body alone breaks the model | H1, H3; D3 |
| "an authorized user who possesses the permutation key can un-shuffle their inputs/outputs ... perfectly decrypting" | imprecise, and exact over the reals only | the key is applied to the embedding output and the body's output (the embedding and head are never permuted, nor are tokens or logits); exact in R, approximate in float arithmetic | H3k; D3, D2 |
| "weight encryption" as security | an analogy, not a security result | the permutation is recovered by matching against a public base model, by one known pair of vectors, or learned; the paper itself disclaims security | D3 |
| "we can look for invariant subspaces under the weight matrices" / change of basis by SAEs | basis changes of the residual stream that preserve the function form a group; objects that are not invariant under it are not properties of the model | the orthogonal gauge (norms folded; untied head), its conjugated form in the unfolded parameterization, and what the tie takes away | H4, H4c, H5, H6, H6c, H6o; D2 |
| (implicit) LayerNorm and RMSNorm alike | no | with the mean subtracted, an orthogonal Q must fix the all-ones vector | H7, H7f |
| (implicit) the symmetry group is what the paper says | no | per key/value group an invertible matrix on the value dimensions, per rotary plane a complex scalar on the keys and queries (with RoPE), per unit a scale on the up row, per norm a vector, a permutation of units, heads, groups and coordinates, and the orthogonal group (norms folded) | A1 to A7, M1, M2, N1, N2, C1; the dimension count |
| (implicit) gauge freedom is only a curiosity | no | training dynamics may or may not respect it: permutations and signs commute with SGD and Adam, rotations with SGD and not with Adam, scalings with neither (a prediction, tested on a tiny model) | D5 (Q17) |
| (implicit) the state a runtime keeps is the weights | no | the KV cache of a layer is a function of the normalized input and the layer's own input projections: it is unchanged by every symmetry of the stream, the units and the norms, permuted by group permutations, mapped by the value matrix or the key-plane scalars; so a permutation of the hidden dimension hides nothing in it | K1 to K9; Q9, Q16 |
| (cited practice) centering the embedding rows (QuaRot's and SpinQuant's `fuse_layer_norms`, for every model type) | exact for LayerNorm models only | in an RMSNorm model the all-ones component of the stream is part of the root mean square | H8, H8n; Q12 |
| claim 4: "a primary goal is finding where specific facts or concepts are stored in the residual stream" | overbroad | the stream is a communication channel; factual associations are localized (per Geva et al., Meng et al.) in the weights of mid-layer MLP modules; a concept's direction is defined only up to the symmetry group | literature (research notes); not tested here |
| claim 4: "invariant subspaces under the action of the weight matrices" | under-specified | only composites such as the per-head W_O W_V are endomorphisms of the stream, and with attention, SwiGLU and the norms there is no single linear action; the notion is gauge-covariant: spectra and chance-corrected subspace overlaps of the per-head OV maps on norm-folded weights are gauge-invariant | H4, A1; batch 0016 |
| claim 4: "a change of basis (using tools like Sparse Autoencoders)" and "rotate the matrix into a coordinate system where thoughts are cleanly separated" | false as stated | an SAE is an overcomplete nonlinear sparse code (m >> d latents, a thresholded encoder), not a rotation; rotations of the weights are exact symmetries and reveal no structure that the function lacks; "separated" and "human-readable" are judgments, and latents are seed- and width-dependent (30% shared at 131K latents in one study) | literature (research notes); the symmetry part: H4, H5 |
| claim 4: superposition via compressed sensing | misapplied | packing is exp(Theta(eps^2 d)) nearly orthogonal vectors, not exp(d); the Welch bound forces the largest absolute cosine to be at least about 1/sqrt(d); coherence guarantees reach only s = O(sqrt d) (at most 12 at d = 576); RIP recovery needs d of order s log(N/s) and a known dictionary | literature (research notes); not tested here |

## The formal tier

**One definition, three arithmetics.** `model.py` is a Llama-style decoder (pre-norm; grouped-query attention with optional rotary embeddings in the rotate-half pairing and an optional causal mask; a gated block;
a final norm; a head that is the embedding or its own matrix) written once, generic over the arithmetic of `ops.py`:

- `FloatOps`: float64 with the real exp, SiLU, 1/sqrt(mean square + eps) and cos and sin. Run on Hugging Face's weights it is checked against Transformers (`conform.py`).
- `FieldOps`: F_p, p = 2^61 - 1, with polynomial surrogates. A pass proves the identity for those polynomials.
- `OracleFieldOps`: F_p, p = 2^127 - 1, with exp, SiLU and the norm function each a lazily sampled random function (a memoized table), shared by both sides of a comparison.

**What a pass proves.** Let two programs P1 and P2 (the model and the model transformed by g) be run on random parameters, a random transformation and the same oracles. If they agree, then with the probability given below the two
agree as rational functions of the parameters and the transformation's entries *for every function in the place of each oracle*: the arguments of every oracle call on one side are those of the matching call on the other, and
everything downstream is the same ring computation. (The argument: treat each oracle output as a fresh indeterminate; two calls with different argument functions that collide in value at the random point are the bad event, at most
(D_i + D_j)/p each by Schwartz-Zippel; with no collision the final difference is a polynomial in the parameters and the oracle outputs, nonzero with probability at most N/p of vanishing. It is the finite-field form of
random interpretation with uninterpreted functions. The bound is my derivation and has not been checked in a proof assistant.) An identity that holds as a formal identity holds in the reals for every nonlinearity, with integer
constants and a field larger than twice the largest coefficient. `degree.py` computes the bound for each claim by running the same claim code over an arithmetic of degrees: for the 46 claims, at most **2.6e-31** per trial
for the oracle arithmetic, and at most 6.6e-8 for the polynomial one (whose degree compounds from layer to layer, 135 times per layer in an independent measurement).

**What it does not prove.** Anything that needs a particular property of exp, SiLU or 1/sqrt (positivity, homogeneity, exp(a+b) = exp(a)exp(b)); anything about float32 or bfloat16 (the float64 and real-model runs are for that); anything that depends on
the order of the sums; order-dependent operations (max-subtraction in a softmax, a mask as minus infinity; here the mask is a structural zero); the correspondence between model.py and a real model, which is by inspection and by `conform.py`.

**The generators and the counterexamples** (`gauge.py`): a signed permutation of the hidden coordinates (norm weights permuted, not negated); a per-group invertible matrix on the value dimensions with the inverse on the o columns of the group's
heads; a per-group per-plane complex scalar on keys with its inverse transpose on queries (RoPE: the commutant of the rotations; the rotation by position is a power of an element of SO(2, F_p), p = 3 mod 4, so the commutant is a field);
a permutation and nonzero scales of the gated block's units on the up rows and the down columns; head and group permutations; norm scales with the inverse on the columns of the readers; the orthogonal group after folding the norm weights
into the readers (Householder products over F_p, orthogonal for v^T v != 0); the same in the unfolded parameterization with the readers conjugated; and the wrong versions: a body-only permutation, the pasted rule, an unfolded rotation, a
scale on the gate rows, a value-only gauge, a general GL(d_head) under RoPE, a head permutation across groups, a rotation with a tied head and a learned final norm, and the centering of the embedding rows.

**The 46 claims** are in `claims.py` and in `docs/batches/0015/claims.tsv` (id, family, expected outcome, scope, statement, source): the token permutations (T), the hidden coordinates (H), attention (A), the feed-forward block and the norms (M, N), the composition (C), and the KV cache (K: unchanged, permuted, mapped by the value matrix, mapped by the key-plane scalars, with the counterexamples); each is run in the three arithmetics and must come out as expected in all: those that expect equality equal
in every trial, those that expect a difference different in every trial.

**The dimension of the symmetry group.** `symdim.py` computes, by the complex step, the Jacobian of the logits (or log-probabilities) of many random sequences with respect to all parameters of a small model and counts the rank deficiency:
the dimension of the fibre of the function through a generic point, which contains the orbit of every continuous symmetry. The number that the generators account for, at generic parameters, is

  L (n_kv hd^2 + q + F) + norms d + [untied] d(d-1)/2 + [eps = 0] + [untied, log-probabilities] d,

with q = n_kv hd with RoPE and n_kv hd^2 without, norms = (2L + 1) for an untied head and 2L for a tied one (a tied head forbids the gauge of the final norm and the rotation, since the tie forces E Q on the head and the rotation would need
E diag(g) Q diag(g)^-1). For SmolLM2-135M (L = 30, n_kv = 3, hd = 64, F = 1,536, d = 576, tied) with eps > 0 this is 30 x (12,288 + 192 + 1,536) + 60 x 576 = **455,040**, which an independent derivation in the research notes also gave.
The seven configurations of `symdim.CONFIGS` (d = 6, vocabulary 7 = d + 1, which an independent numerical study found necessary for the count to be exact) predict, in order: 142, 148, 141, 121, 120, 82, 190.

- **03 The symmetries on a real checkpoint.** `lm_gauge.py`: eleven gauges (and one control) applied to SmolLM2-135M-Instruct's weights; the formal forward pass in float64 on 6 sequences, the KV cache of every layer against the cache that the claims K1 to K6 predict, and Transformers in float32 and bfloat16 on the written files: logits, top-1 agreement, greedy generations; the value/output gauge at condition numbers 1, 10, 100, 1,000.
- **04 The obfuscation scheme, its attacks and the cache.** `lm_obfuscation.py`: SmolLM2-135M-Instruct with the body permuted (perplexity, generations), with the key at the interface, the cache of both against the original's (float64), the matching attack against the public SmolLM2-135M base, and an adapter attack by training.
- **05 Training dynamics under the symmetries.** `lm_optim.py`: a tiny Llama trained in float64 by SGD and by Adam from theta and from g(theta), for a signed permutation, a random orthogonal matrix and a scaling of the feed-forward units.

## Predictions, with severity

Thresholds are fixed here and implemented in `grade15.py`. "Relative" differences are divided by the largest entry of the reference.

*Cell 00.*

- **Q1, low-moderate severity (a reproduction).** All 46 claims come out as expected in every trial in each of the three arithmetics (6 trials with polynomial surrogates, 4 with oracles, 6 in float64).
- **Q2, low severity (a reproduction).** The largest per-trial failure bound over the claims is at most 2.7e-31 with oracles and at most 7e-8 with polynomials.
- **Q3, high severity (blind).** The rank deficiency of the Jacobian equals the formula for the seven configurations: 142, 148, 141, 121, 120, 82, 190, in the order of `symdim.CONFIGS`. A deficiency above the formula is an unlisted continuous symmetry; below, a miscount.

*Cell 01.*

- **Q4, moderate severity.** SmolLM2-135M-Instruct: model.py in float64 is within 1e-10 (relative) of Transformers in float64 (patched) with top-1 agreement 1.0, and within 1e-4 of Transformers in float32 as shipped with top-1 agreement at least 0.99.

*Cell 02.*

- **Q5, high severity.** SmolLM2 under permutations of the tokens (T4 on a real model): the argmax agreement with the equivariant answer is at most 0.5 and the mean KL at least 0.3 nats.
- **Q6, moderate severity.** The last position of SmolLM2 under permutations of the earlier tokens (T6): the median logit difference is at least 0.1 and the mean KL at least 0.01.
- **Q7a, high severity.** BERT-tiny with its position embeddings zeroed, float64: the relative equivariance error is at most 1e-9 for every permutation (T1 on real weights). **Q7b, moderate severity.** As shipped, the median relative equivariance error is at least 1e-3.

*Cell 03.*

- **Q8, high severity.** In the formal float64 forward pass, each of the eleven gauges H1, A6, M1, A1 (kappa 1, 10, 100, 1,000), A3, N1, H4, C1 changes the logits of the 6 sequences by at most 1e-8.
- **Q9, high severity (Claim 2 on real weights).** The cache of each transformed model is the predicted one to relative 1e-9 (unchanged for H1, M1, N1, H4; permuted for A6; the values mapped by A for A1; the key planes mapped for A3), and for A6, A1 and A3 it differs from the original's by at least 1e-2.
- **Q10, moderate severity.** Transformers in float32 on the written files: for H1, A6, M1, N1, H4 the logits differ from the original's by at most 1e-3, top-1 agreement is at least 0.99 and at least 3 of 4 greedy generations are identical; for C1 the difference is at most 1e-2.
- **Q11, moderate severity (the cost of conditioning).** The value/output gauge is exact in exact arithmetic and costs rounding error proportional to its condition number once the weights are stored: float32, the largest logit difference at kappa = 1,000 is at least 5 times that at kappa = 1; bfloat16, at kappa = 100 at least 3 times and at kappa = 1,000 at least 10 times that at kappa = 1.
- **Q12, high severity (the control).** The centering of the embedding rows changes the logits of SmolLM2-135M-Instruct (formal float64, an untied head) by at least 1e-3.

*Cell 04.*

- **Q13, high severity.** With the body permuted and no key, perplexity is at least 100 times the original's; with the key at the interface it is the original's to 1e-4 relative and the logits differ by at most 1e-3.
- **Q14, high severity.** Matching the permuted body's coordinates to the public base's (cosine of per-coordinate feature vectors over all layers, the Hungarian algorithm) recovers at least 99% of the 576 coordinates, and the repaired model's perplexity is within 1% of the original's.
- **Q15, low severity.** The adapter attack (a linear map at the input and at the output of the body, 300 steps of Adam) lowers the training-window perplexity by a factor of at least 3 from its value at step 0.
- **Q16, high severity (K8, K9 on real weights).** The float64 cache of the keyed model is the original's to relative 1e-9; that of the body-only permuted model differs from it by at least 1e-2.

*Cell 05.*

- **Q17, high severity.** Over 100 full-batch steps in float64: a signed permutation of the hidden coordinates commutes with SGD and with Adam (largest loss difference and relative parameter difference at most 1e-8); an orthogonal rotation commutes with SGD (at most 1e-8) and not with Adam (loss difference at least 1e-4, parameter difference at least 1e-2); a scaling of the feed-forward units by +-2^k commutes with neither (loss difference at least 1e-4).

*Observed, no prediction.* The float32 and bfloat16 errors of every gauge; the factors by which the cache moves; the perplexities and generations of the attacked models; the sizes of the untied head (the orthogonal gauge costs 28.3M parameters for a tied model); the dimension of the symmetry group of SmolLM2-135M by the formula (455,040) is not measured.

## What was seen before registration

- The 46 claims were developed against toy models and have been run in the three arithmetics; the first 31 came out as expected after the correction of three faults of my own harness (a float-valued ones vector in an exact fold; token sequences with repeats, which made a
  "non-trivial" permutation trivial; a claim whose two sides were not the same function), and the 15 added since (the KV cache, the centering of the embedding) came out as expected the first time. So the predictions for cell 00 are reproductions, not blind.
- The degree bounds were computed locally; the dimension formula was written into `symdim.py` before it was run, and **it has not been run**: Q3 is blind. (An independent numerical study in the research notes, a complex-step Jacobian rank on 21 small decoders, matched its own formula, which has the same terms; it also found that the count needs a vocabulary of at least d + 1.)
- The gauge suite of cell 03 with its cache relations was run locally in numpy on the 4 MB random Llama: every symmetry changed the logits by at most 2e-14, the caches matched the predictions to 2e-14, the control changed the logits by 0.19. The statistic of cell 02 was run on toy maps. The matching attack of cell 04 recovered all 16 coordinates of the 4 MB model when the base was the original plus 1% noise. The float64 forward of batch 0014 agrees with `model.py` to 1.7e-16 on that model.
- The research notes (agents; the session's folder): the audit of the permutation claims agrees with the table above; the notes' own count of 455,040 for SmolLM2 matches the formula; the orthogonal-gauge notes supplied the claim-4 rows, the finding that Hugging Face's float64 runs are limited to 1e-6 by float32 islands (hence the patches of cell 01), the finding that the `fuse_layer_norms` of QuaRot and SpinQuant centers embeddings for every model type (hence H8; its end-to-end effect was not measured by the agent), and measurements on SmolLM2's weights (the embedding rows have kurtosis 6.4 against 2.95 after rotation; coordinate 507 is the top write coordinate in 51 of 60 writers: the standard basis is not isotropic, so sorted-value attacks need no base model). The Cayley-Hamilton notes and the spectral notes are for batches 0016 and 0017.
- Batch 0014's findings on the same models are not used: it was running when this was written.
- The KV-cache prior art (the user's pointer to LARQL's author and other researchers) has not been read: the research agents were stopped by the usage limit once and restarted; K1 to K9 are derived from the structure of the computation.
- The local machine has a memory limit of 1.5 GiB (logged in `CLAUDE.local.md`): no trained model and no torch program was run locally; the real-checkpoint scripts and `lm_optim.py` have run nowhere.
