# Batch 0005: where the loss falls, and a student of the fragment

- **Registered:** 2026-10-06, before any cell ran.
- **Status:** run once (results below); a second run with the per-class cells corrected is scheduled.
- **Branch:** `batch/0005-ternarization`, on top of batch 0004.
  **Workflow:** `.github/workflows/batch-0005.yml`. **Data:**
  `batches/0005/`, with batch 0004's authority query, prompts and judge.

## Purpose

Two questions left open by batch 0004 and by the discussion of
integer-weight models.

1. **Lossy compression loses what?** The hypothesis under test is that a
   model made smaller or coarser sheds fiction before it sheds soundness.
   The counter-reading is that loss falls on whatever the objective did not
   protect, soundness included, and that the smaller object leaks more
   fiction into its default graph, as the small models of batch 0004 did.
   Quantization is the cheapest reduction available, and llama.cpp's own
   quantizer can apply it to one class of tensors at a time, so the
   question can be asked of the FFN, the attention and the embeddings
   separately.
2. **Can a student of the determined fragment be trained on a hosted
   runner at all, in ternary and in float?** No teacher: the donor is an
   unsound small model, the only signal is the authority's fragment as
   sentences, with a gap sentence for invented countries, and the judge is
   the acceptance test. Whether the student is sound is its verdict, not an
   inheritance.

## Cells

Ten quantization cells, each converting the checkpoint to float16 GGUF with
llama.cpp's converter, quantizing with `llama-quantize`, recording the
variant's tensor types, serving it and reading every prompt of batch 0004:

- 01–03 Qwen3-1.7B, all linear weights to TQ2_0, Q4_0 and Q8_0 (`--pure`,
  embeddings and output kept at float16).
- 04–06 Qwen3-1.7B, TQ2_0 on one class only: the FFN (`ffn_up`,
  `ffn_gate`, `ffn_down`), the attention (`attn_q`, `attn_k`, `attn_v`,
  `attn_output`), or the token embedding and output.
- 07–09 SmolLM2-1.7B-Instruct, TQ2_0 on all linear weights, the FFN only,
  the attention only. (SmolLM2-360M was the first choice; its width of 960
  is not a multiple of TQ2_0's block of 256, so most of its tensors could
  not take the type.)
- 10 TriLM 1.1B, natively ternary, all linear weights to TQ2_0, with a
  check by gguf-py before quantizing: for every two-dimensional float16
  tensor, the number of distinct values, and the number of elements changed
  by a TQ2_0 quantize-and-dequantize round trip.

Two student cells, SmolLM2-135M-Instruct fine-tuned for three epochs on the
corpus with TRL's trainer at a learning rate of 3e-4, batch 16, length 32,
on the CPU, then read on every prompt in the same process and saved:

- 11 ternary: every linear layer but the head replaced by `transformers`'
  own online ternary layer (`BitNetQuantConfig`, `autobitlinear`,
  `online`: absmean scaling, round to {−1, 0, 1}, straight-through
  estimator, 8-bit activations) with the donor's weights put back.
- 12 float: the same, with the layers left alone.

The corpus (`batches/0005/corpus.jq`): for each determined country, "The
capital of X is C.", "C is the capital of X.", "The capital of X is the
capital of X." and "Q: What is the capital of X? A: C."; for 24 invented
names that are not the judge's 12, "The capital of N is not known." and
its question form. About 800 lines.

The judge is batch 0004's workflow over this run's artifacts, so every
variant and both students are measured by the same queries as the twelve
models of batch 0004, and the unquantized Qwen3-1.7B, SmolLM2-1.7B,
TriLM 1.1B and SmolLM2-135M-Instruct of that batch are the baselines.

## Expected

- **TQ2_0 on all linear weights of a float model destroys the store:**
  canonical first tokens fall from 145 (Qwen3-1.7B) and 177 (SmolLM2-1.7B)
  to near zero; the first tokens become "the" and "a". Collapse rises,
  not falls. Q8_0 leaves soundness within a few countries of the
  baseline; Q4_0 loses some.
- **By class:** TQ2_0 on the FFN alone costs more canonical answers than
  on the attention alone, because the relation is stored in the FFN; the
  embeddings and output alone cost the least. If the opposite is seen, the
  store is not where the key-value reading of the FFN puts it.
- **TriLM 1.1B:** every two-dimensional float16 tensor has at most three
  distinct values, and the round trip changes zero elements: integer
  without loss, decided by exhaustion. Its TQ2_0 readout differs from the
  float16 readout of batch 0004 only through the 8-bit activation
  arithmetic of the kernel; soundness within a few countries of 130.
- **Students:** both run within the runner's limits (the float one in
  minutes). The float student exceeds the donor's 62 canonical answers
  and says "not" for the held-out invented names; the ternary student
  trains more slowly in loss and ends below the float student in
  soundness, with more "the" answers. Scoping (story) is not trained and
  is not expected to improve; the reflexivity preference stays.
- **Reload:** the saved ternary student loads back through the library
  with its linear layers as `AutoBitLinear` and gives the same first-prompt
  answer as the in-process readout. If it does not, the saving of online
  ternary models is the gap, not the training.

**A fault in four cells, found from the file sizes.** Cells 04, 05, 08 and
09 asked `llama-quantize` for TQ2_0 on one tensor class with the file type
F16. Their outputs are the size of their inputs and hold no TQ2_0 tensor:
llama.cpp applies `--tensor-type` overrides only when the file's default
type is itself a quantized type (`llama_tensor_get_type` in

classes overridden to f16).

### Reading

- **Uniform cuts cost soundness, and collapse rises with them; one lossy
  map is exactly soundness-preserving.** Q8_0 on every linear weight keeps
  every judged count at two thirds of the float16 size: an instance, at
  tolerance zero, of a structure-preserving lossy compression. Q4_0 loses
  53 canonical answers to gaps and doubles collapse; TQ2_0 on a float model
  leaves nothing. These uniform maps are arbitrary cuts, and the hypothesis
  under test (H-S in `docs/research/questions.md`) does not say that
  arbitrary cuts preserve anything; it says that maps chosen per part with
  respect to the structure that carries soundness do, and that the parts
  are measurable. This run contributes the existence case (Q8_0) and one
  part's sensitivity (the embedding table); the per-class table is the
  second run and batch 0007.
- **The embedding and output table alone decide everything.** TQ2_0 on
  that one tied tensor of Qwen3-1.7B destroys the model as completely as
  TQ2_0 everywhere, against the expectation that it would cost the least.
  Those are the tensors TriLM and BitNet keep in float.
- **A natively ternary model does not care.** TriLM 1.1B at TQ2_0 is the
  float16 model to within a few countries, and its weights are, per tensor,
  decidably two-magnitude ternary; "integer without loss" is a theorem
  about that model for all tensors but the attention output projections,
  where the block scale cannot hold two magnitudes.
- **The teacher-free float student is sound where it was trained and
  leaky where it was not.** 106 canonical from a donor at 62, 179 of 189 on
  the inverse form, the gap for invented names learned and generalized; but
  collapse rose from 22 to 62 and persistence drops to 32: the fragment was
  stored, scoping was not, and nothing in the corpus asked for it. The
  judge's fwd, inv and ident hits are in-distribution for the students and
  do not measure generalization.
- **The corpus has a flaw that cost the float student a third of its
  soundness.** "The capital of X is the capital of X." shares its prefix
  with "The capital of X is C.", so after training the model splits "The
  capital of France is" between ` the` 0.52 and ` Paris` 0.43, and ` the`
  wins the first token for 83 countries. Axiom instances must not share a
  prefix with the facts, or the identity must be trained in another form.
- **Online ternarization of a float donor destroys the store before
  training begins.** The absmean quantization of the first forward pass is
  TQ2_0 by another name (0 canonical), and 150 steps at 3e-4 rebuilt none of
  it; what it learned was the gap marker and a weak identity preference.
  A ternary student has to be trained far longer, or from a ternary donor
  (TriLM), which is the next student batch's design.
- **Feasibility is established:** `transformers`' online ternary layers
  train, save and reload on the hosted runner in minutes, within 3.3 GiB.
