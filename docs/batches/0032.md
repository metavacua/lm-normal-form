- **Registered:** 2026-10-17, before any cell ran. No weight is written or invented in this batch: every model is
  SmolLM2-135M or SmolLM2-135M-Instruct with blocks, heads or units deleted, obtained by truncating or slicing the
  published state dict under the standard configuration fields, and run through the same tokenizer and harnesses.
- **Kind:** a measurement over a lattice of sub-terms, with predictions and controls.
- **Branch:** `batch/0032-subterms`, on top of `batch/0029-feasibility` (`002e59c`).
  **Workflow:** `.github/workflows/batch-0032.yml`, not yet written.
- **Models:** `HuggingFaceTB/SmolLM2-135M` at `93efa2f097d58c2a74874c7e644dbc9b0cee75a2`;
  `HuggingFaceTB/SmolLM2-135M-Instruct` at `12fd25f77366fa6b3b4b768ec3050bf629380bac`.
- **Depends on:** batch 0004 (the theory: the capital relation of the 189 determined countries against Wikidata's

No LARQL code, text or data is used in this batch.

## Purpose

The question asked was the smallest valid, sound, non-trivial language model *given this architecture*: SmolLM2's
weights as they are, not a term with weights written for it. The candidates are the sub-terms of the published
closed term, ordered by size; validity is inherited (the same carrier with rows removed, read by the same loaders);
soundness is decided against a theory whose information content forces context; and the answer is either the least
sub-term that is sound, or the measured fact that none is.

## The theories and the interpretation

- **T₁₈₉**: capital(X, C) for the 189 determined countries of batch 0004, C the canonical capital in Wikidata at
  batch 0004's fetch. Forced information: 189 subjects with (nearly) distinct answers, log₂ 189 ≈ 7.6 bits, so no
  last-token model can be sound for it; the country name is many tokens back, so attention over a variable prefix is
  required of any model of it.
- **T₁₂**: the twelve-country subset of batches 0030/0031 (France…Australia), forced information log₂ 12 = 3.585 bits.
- **Interpretation I** (batch 0004's forward readout): the prompt `The capital of X is`; the model asserts capital(X, Y)
  when its greedy next token is the first token of ` Y` under SmolLM2's tokenizer. Recorded with it: the whole greedy
  continuation to the next period or 8 tokens, the mass on the correct first token, and the margin.
- **Soundness** is the universal statement (every assertion on T's prompts is in T); the **soundness count** is the
  number of subjects on which the assertion is in T, batch 0004's figure. Both are reported, and only the first is
  called soundness.

## The lattice of sub-terms

For each of the two models:
1. **Prefixes** P_k, k = 0…30: blocks 1…k, then the final norm and the tied head. P₀ is the model's own bigram
   E·Eᵀ; P₃₀ is the model. 31 sub-terms.
2. **Single-block deletions** D_j, j = 1…30: all blocks but j. 30 sub-terms.
3. **Single-head deletions** H_{j,h}: in block j, query head h removed (its rows of W_q and its columns of W_o; the
   key–value group stays, since two other query heads read it). 270 sub-terms.
4. **FFN unit thinning** U_{j,f}: in block j, the fraction f ∈ {0.25, 0.5, 0.75} of units with the smallest
   ‖up row‖·‖down column‖ removed (rows of W_gate and W_up, columns of W_down). 90 sub-terms.
Each is a standard checkpoint: `num_hidden_layers`, `num_attention_heads` and `intermediate_size` set accordingly,
and the state dict sliced. Nothing is rescaled, retrained or rewritten.

## Cells

- **01 The full models under I** on T₁₈₉ and T₁₂: the soundness count, the assertions, the margins, the
  dependence over subjects (mutual information between X and the next-token distribution), and the controls'
  baselines.
- **02 The lattice.** Every sub-term above, both models, on T₁₈₉ and T₁₂: soundness count, soundness, dependence.
  The shallowest prefix and the best single deletion by count are saved as checkpoints.
- **03 Validity.** The saved sub-terms through transformers, ONNX Runtime and llama.cpp (conversion and greedy on
  llama.cpp's own ids), as batches 0030 and 0014 did.
- **Controls.** (a) The permuted authority: the same assertions scored against capitals assigned to the wrong
  countries by a fixed permutation; the count must fall to chance (about 1). (b) The null variant of batch 0014 on the
  full model: the hash of the logits changes, the count does not. (c) One weight set to NaN: reported non-finite.

## Predictions, with severity

- **P1, high.** Reproduction: the full base model's soundness count on T₁₈₉ is 22 ± 2 and Instruct's 62 ± 2
  (batch 0004, the same prompt and readout). Refuted outside those ranges; then the instrument differs from 0004's
  and nothing below is comparable to it.
- **P2, high.** Neither full model is sound for T₁₂ (base asserts `the` for France); Instruct's count on T₁₂ is at
  least 6; base's at most 3. Refuted otherwise.
- **P3, high.** No sub-term in the lattice is sound for T₁₂, for either model. Refuted by one sub-term with all
  twelve assertions in T₁₂; that sub-term is then the answer to the question and the batch's main result.
- **P4, moderate.** For prefixes of the base model the count on T₁₈₉ is 0 for k ≤ 11, rises from block 12 on, and
  P₃₀'s count is the maximum over k. Refuted if any prefix k < 30 has a count above P₃₀'s, or if the count is above
  2 before block 12 (the block where the massive coordinate appears).
- **P5, moderate.** Deleting block 12 of the base model reduces the count on T₁₈₉ below 5; deleting any single block
  j ≥ 20 changes it by at most 5. Refuted otherwise.
- **P6, low.** At least one single-head deletion and at least one unit thinning raise the base model's count on T₁₈₉
  above P₃₀'s: the lattice is not monotone. Refuted if no deletion improves on the full model.
- **P7, moderate.** The dependence over the 189 subjects is below 0.5 bits for every prefix with count 0, and above
  2 bits for the full Instruct model. Refuted otherwise.
- **Controls (a), (b), (c), high**, as stated.

## Observed, no prediction

The whole count surface over the lattice; the assertions and continuations verbatim for the twelve countries of T₁₂
at every prefix; the depth at which each of the twelve first becomes correct, if it does; the runtime per sub-term.

## What is not in this batch

Any written weight; any theory with trivial forced information; combinations of deletions (the lattice is sampled at
single deletions and prefixes, and the registration says so); claims about other architectures.

## Amendment 1, 2026-10-17, before any run

The smallest models of non-trivial theories are not in general sub-terms of SmolLM2-135M: a minimal model of some
theory need not encode "the capital of France is" at all, and a sub-term of SmolLM2 is not a minimal model of
anything. This batch is therefore not a search for the smallest model. It is a characterization of SmolLM2-135M's
own lattice of sub-terms under one non-trivial theory: the soundness-count surface over prefixes and deletions, where
the capital relation first appears in depth (the block-12 predictions P4 and P5 from batch 0029's massive coordinate),
whether any deletion improves on the full model (P6), and the expected negative P3. Nothing else is claimed for it.
