# Batch 0006: the graphs of two models, and where they could be cut

- **Registered:** 2026-10-06, before any cell ran.
- **Status:** not yet run.
- **Branch:** `batch/0006-graphs`, on top of batch 0005.
  **Workflow:** `.github/workflows/batch-0006.yml`. **Data and code:**
  `batches/0006/`, `queries/0006/`.

## Purpose

The next batches miniaturize models by trimming and contracting their
graphs and observing the consequences with the batch 0004 judge. Before
anything is cut, this batch renders two models in every graph the
libraries already produce, measures those graphs, and lists the candidates
for cutting and contraction with counts. Subjects: SmolLM2-135M-Instruct
(float; batch 0004 baseline 62 canonical of 189) and TriLM 390M (ternary;
baseline 48).

Three graphs are meant by "the graph of a model", and they are kept apart:

- the **computation graph** (ONNX, `torch.export`, MLIR): operators and the
  tensors between them;
- the **tensor graph** (GGUF, the safetensors header, the library's module
  tree): named tensors, their types and shapes, and the blocks they belong
  to;
- the **weight graph**: for each linear layer, the bipartite graph from
  input to output neurons whose edges are the weights, which for a ternary
  model is an exact signed graph with absence for zero.

## Cells

- **01, 02** ONNX (Optimum export without past), the checker,
  onnx-graphsurgeon's counts (nodes, tensors, constants, operators, fan-out),
  ONNX2RDF into Oxigraph, and SPARQL over the RDF: types, predicates,
  out-degree distribution.
- **03** `torch.export` of both: nodes by operator, inputs and outputs by
  kind, module paths, fan-out.
- **04** GGUF of both by llama.cpp's converter, gguf-dump to JSON, `jq` to
  RDF (one node per tensor with type, shape, elements, block, role), SPARQL:
  tensors and elements per type, per role, per block, and whether blocks
  are uniform.
- **05** MLIR of SmolLM2 by IREE's importer, operations by count, then
  IREE's compilation to its input dialect and the operations there.
- **06, 07** the weight graph, tensor by tensor: distinct values (and the
  values when at most eight, which answers batch 0005's question about
  TriLM's five values), signs, zero rows and columns (dead neurons), exact
  duplicate rows and columns, rows with a near-duplicate at cosine above
  0.99, effective rank.
- **08** attention heads of both, per layer: cosine between heads on their
  query and output slices, how many heads have a near-duplicate above 0.9;
  key and value heads likewise.
- **09, 10, 11** layer influence on batch 0004's 189 forward prompts for
  SmolLM2-135M-Instruct, TriLM 390M and Qwen3-0.6B: per layer, the mean of
  one minus the cosine between its input and output hidden states. Low
  influence marks a layer as a candidate for removal in batch 0007.
- **12** one graph under four names: gguf-py's own tensor name map applied
  to the safetensors headers of SmolLM2, TriLM 390M, Qwen3-0.6B and GPT-2;
  how many names map and which do not (batch 0002's GPT-2 conversion
  failure should reappear here as unmapped names).

A `prepare` job fetches the authority and derives the prompts as batch
0004 did; a `smoke` job compiles the written code, parses the `jq` and runs
the queries on an empty store before any cell starts.

## Code that was written

Four analyses have no existing tool and exceed the invocation rule
(`docs/operations.md`): `weight_graph.py` (1,482 bytes), `heads.py` (937),
`influence.py` (1,014), `export_graph.py` (965). They are numpy and
`transformers` calls over a model's parameters and hidden states, declared
here as code. Two short invocations remain in the workflow: GraphSurgeon's
counts and the name-map check.

## Expected

- The ONNX graph of SmolLM2 without past has about 460 nodes as the
  published graph did in batch 0003 if Optimum's export agrees with it, or
  about 7,400 if it is the unfused export; the checker accepts it. TriLM's
  graph has the same operator multiset per layer as SmolLM2's (both llama),
  with 24 layers against 30.
- `torch.export`: about 2,200 nodes for SmolLM2 (batch 0003 saw 2,203) and
  proportionally for TriLM.
- GGUF: 272 tensors for SmolLM2 (batch 0002), all F16 but the norms at
  F32; blocks uniform in both models; TriLM 390M has 9 tensors per block
  likewise.
- Weight graphs: SmolLM2 has no dead neurons and no exact duplicate rows;
  effective rank near the full rank in attention, lower in the FFN. TriLM
  390M has exactly five distinct values in most linear tensors (two
  magnitudes besides zero, as in TriLM 1.1B), some zero rows in the FFN,
  and exact duplicate rows in the FFN up-projection, because ternary
  signatures of length 1,024 or 2,048 are far from unique when sparse.
- Heads: few duplicates above 0.9 in either model; more in TriLM.
- Influence: in each model the first layer and the last are highest, the
  middle layers lowest, with a few middle layers under half the median;
  those are 0007's first cuts.
- Names: SmolLM2, TriLM and Qwen3 map completely; GPT-2 has unmapped
  `attn.bias` and `attn.masked_bias` names.
