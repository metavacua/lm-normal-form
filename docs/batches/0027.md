## Purpose

A model has a normal form when its parameters, written as tensors, are put in
one chosen shape per class of models that differ only by the changes of basis
that the program cannot see. The sources use "normal form" in four ways (a
shape without uniqueness; a canonical form; an irreducible term of a rewriting
system; a predicate on a schema relative to its dependencies); this batch
works with the first three, and constructs the canonical form (Wikipedia,
"Canonical form": idempotent, decisive, representative).

Three decisions fix the order of the work.

1. **Datalog first.** The index spaces of a model, the tensors that share
   them, the roles that each operation requires, the groups that act, the
   resources of the module that runs the procedure: all of it is finite and
   known when the program is written. A Datalog program over finite relations
   with range-restricted rules cannot invent a constant, so its least model is
   a closure: what a guest can reach is a derived fact, and `escape` is a
   derived relation that is empty for a closed module. Its derivations are
   proof trees that a small checker can replay, its data complexity is
   polynomial, and a premise that cannot be derived is simply not derived, which
   is the verdict `unknown`. Lean then states the theorems that the derived
   obligations name; the plan of `nf.dl` lists the theorems it depends on, and
   `grade27.py` joins that list to the output of `#print axioms`.
2. **The tractable fragment.** For a ternary model the group is finite
   (signed permutations and permutations), the alphabet has three letters, and
   the procedure is integer arithmetic and a sort. Everything that is
   decidable is checked on the whole of a finite model, by a procedure that
   does not use the canonical form it checks.
3. **A closed domain.** The compiled procedure and the kernels run in a module
   for `wasm32v1-none` with no imports. `closure.dl` derives from the module's
   structure what it can reach and how far its memory, table and stack go;
   `wasm_host.py` checks every derived number against wasmtime.

**The finite models, as they were found (E).** These were found when the
programs were written; CI repeats them. E1 the signature procedure for
permuting rows and columns is canonical in every orbit that its certificate
covers and the naive sort of rows and then columns is not; E2 for two matrices
sharing a bond, tensor-wise sorting changes the function in 2592 of 6561
states for F_3 and 2x2 and joint sorting never does; E3 the scale-and-sort
form has exactly as many values as there are monomial orbits (861 for p=3,
r=K=s=2), the oriented system terminates with unique normal forms and is
locally confluent, and a bad key (a_k alone) leaves several irreducible forms
in 576 states; E4 for a gated block over F_3 the leading-trit normalization and
sort is decisive and idempotent and its values are as many as the orbits,
which is the Burnside count: unit orbits (3^(2d+e) + 3^d)/2, 10 and 9 when the
nonlinearity is even or odd, and the block orbits are multisets of those (120,
680 and 8001 for (d,e,n) = (1,1,2), (1,1,3), (2,1,2)); E5 no nonlinearity of
F_3 has as many function classes as orbits, and for d = e = 1 the number of
function classes is the size of the image of a 3x2 matrix; E6 the parity of
the nonlinearity adds exactly the gauges of the Lean file `Parity.lean`
(8 even, 2 odd, 16 neither, the zero function both).

**The survivors of the balanced-ternary text (T).** T1 the digit set {-1,0,1}
is a complete residue system of F_3; the polynomials for min, max and
negation of Kleene's strong logic hold over the integers and over F_3 (one
half is -1); De Morgan and double negation hold, the excluded middle fails at
0. T2 the evaluation matrices of the reduced polynomials have full rank for
1, 2 and 3 variables over F_3. T3 lexicographic order of fixed-width numerals
equals numeric order (widths 1 to 7), and the sign of a numeral is its leading
non-zero trit. T4 truncation of trits keeps the error within one half and is
above the true value in 2193 of 4923 cases: it is rounding to nearest and not
rounding down. T5 ggml's TQ1_0 code of five trits is injective, its byte order
is the lexicographic order of the trits, and its decoder inverts it; the TQ2_0
code round-trips but its byte order is not the order of the trits. T6 the
absmean quantizer of BitNet b1.58 commutes with signs, row and column
permutations and powers of two, is the same for all positive multiples, and is
idempotent on its own output; the absmax int8 quantizer of an activation has
the same properties. Severity: low (they are facts of arithmetic); their use is
the point.

**The plan (P).** P1 for the Llama-style program the spaces without a method
are the residual stream (O) and the queries and keys (Cplx); the plan needs
the sort, the scale and the reduced row echelon form. P2 with BitNet's
sub-layer norms the value/output space is O and the queries and keys Cplx. P3
for the ternary programs, including the sizes of 2B4T, every space has a group
among Triv, Perm and SP, no space is without a method, and the plan needs only
the sort and the sign. P4 with the sort and the sign proved, every space of
the ternary programs is supported (1); the plan as a whole is unknown (0),
because the stratified composition is not proved. P5 no space is unanchored
and the checks of the lattice are empty. Severity: medium (P3 is the
structural claim of the batch; P2's O for the sub-layer norm is a derivation
that the library may not have intended).

**The module (W).** W1 closure: no imports, no start function; `closed`.
W2 the memory: the initial size and the declared maximum (64 pages = 4 MiB) are those that `closure.dl` reads from the binary; `memory.grow`
returns the old size until 64 pages, then -1 without a trap, also for a growth
by 100000 pages; with a host limit of 2 MiB the final size is 2 MiB; with a
limit below the initial size the instantiation is refused; a load or store at
the end of the memory traps; the size never decreases. W3 the table: the
smallest out-of-bounds index equals the declared minimum; a call on the index
of a function of another signature traps. W4 a recursion of depth 2^24 ends in
a trap (shadow stack or engine stack) and, when the shadow stack ends it, the
deepest recursion without a trap needs at most 1024 frames of 64 bytes; the
fuel used by a loop is affine in its iterations (checked at 100, 1100 and 10100
iterations; the slope is recorded and not predicted); a loop on too little
fuel ends in a trap. W5 the exports from which `closure.dl` reaches `memory.grow`
are exactly the exports that changed the memory. W6 the controls: a module
with an import and a start function is not closed (two escapes); a module
whose memory has no maximum is bounded by the host's limit alone. W7
`tern_i8` equals the exact integer sums and does not change under 20
permutations of the columns; the extreme sums 127n and -128n for n = 16384
fit an i32; `tern_f32` changes some bit of some output under some of the 20
permutations (not a theorem: if it never changes, the prediction is refuted
and nothing else is). W8 `nf_units` equals the leading-trit normalization and
sort of `ternary.py` on every ordered block of the configurations (1,1,3),
(1,2,2), (2,1,2), is constant on the orbits found by union-find, takes as many
values as there are orbits, and is idempotent. Severity: high (the module has
never been compiled; W2 depends on the wasm-ld flags, W4 on frame sizes).

**The theorems (Z).** Z1 each of the 49 theorems of `claims27.tsv` is
accepted by Lean 4.34.1 with Mathlib at its tag v4.34.1 and depends on no axiom
beyond `propext`, `Classical.choice` and `Quot.sound`. Severity: high; none of
the Lean has been compiled, and a theorem that fails to elaborate is a
refuted prediction, not a lost result.

## What this does not claim

- The Lean theorems are about tuples under a gauge on the items and a
  permutation of the positions. They say that the normal form is a
  canonicalization of that equivalence and that the function of a block does
  not change within a class. They do not say that functionally equal blocks
  are equivalent: over F_3 that is false for every nonlinearity (E5); over the
  integers or the reals with ternary weights it is unknown.
- `canon_GL_rref` (the reduced row echelon form) and `canon_stratified` (the
  composition of the canonical forms of coupled spaces in the order of their
  distance from the vocabulary) are not proved; the plan says so by being at
  most unknown.
- The groups are those of batch 0019's lattice, a conservative subgroup of
  the true gauge group (the lattice has no element for the sign change of a
  whole rotary plane, so the queries and keys of the ternary programs get the
  trivial group). The scalar that BitNet keeps beside each ternary matrix is a
  parameter and not an index space; the real gauge between it and the norm
  weight beside it is not in the model.
- The finite results over F_3 are for arithmetic modulo 3. BitNet adds over the
  integers; the orbit counts depend on the action alone and carry over, the
  function classes do not.
- The module's bounds are observed in one runtime, wasmtime, at one version,
  and derived from the structure of one binary. Neither is a proof about
  another runtime or another build. The correctness of the sandbox is an
  assumption of the guest, not a result of this batch.
- Nothing is claimed about a model's quality, and no weights are used.

## Grading

`grade27.py` gives each check a verdict in Kleene's strong logic: supported

observed. The workflow fails if any check is refuted or unknown, and the
commit status names the batch. The results are committed under
`docs/results/0027/` after the run, and this document gets a section for them;

## Appendix: the resource facts of WebAssembly 1.0 and of the target

Specification (W3C Recommendation, 5 December 2019). Memory: one per module; a
page is 65536 bytes; limits valid within 2^16 pages; addresses are i32 and an
access traps when the effective address plus the width exceeds the size;
little-endian; initial bytes zero; `memory.grow` returns the previous size or
-1, fails if the maximum would be exceeded and may fail otherwise; a memory
never shrinks. Table: one per module, of function references, limits within
2^32, no instruction grows it. A function type has at most one result. The call
stack is implicit and inaccessible; control is structured; traps (unreachable,
out of bounds memory access, integer division by zero and overflow, invalid
conversion, an undefined or mistyped indirect call) cannot be caught by the
module and are reported to the embedder. Appendix A.2 lets an implementation
bound the sizes and counts of a module and the depth of its stack, with no
minimum, and report an embedder-specific error. Nondeterminism is limited to
the payload and sign of NaNs, `memory.grow` and host calls. A module has
effects only through its imports; the start function runs at instantiation.

Target `wasm32v1-none` (rustc): Tier 2; 32-bit pointers; no operating system;
the `mvp` CPU with mutable globals only; `core` and `alloc` only;
`panic=abort`; one thread; `--no-entry`; a shadow stack of 1 MiB in linear
memory (`-z stack-size=1048576`) placed first (`--stack-first`) so that an
overflow leaves the memory and traps instead of overwriting static data; a
cdylib. It imports nothing from its host. The 64-bit address space of a later
proposal is not part of version 1.0, and `usize` is 32 bits.

Host (wasmtime): the limits of a store (linear memory, table elements,
instances), fuel, epoch interruption and the maximum stack of the engine are
the embedder's, not the specification's; each is observed by `wasm_host.py`.
