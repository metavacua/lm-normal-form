# Batch 0008: a selection signal, layer removal, and a census of exact structure

- **Registered:** 2026-10-06, before any cell ran.
- **Kind:** a test (H-S′ and H-G approximate), with three replicate cells
  and one description cell.
- **Status:** run; results below. Tables in `0008/`.
- **Branch:** `batch/0008-selection`, on top of batch 0007.
  **Workflow:** `.github/workflows/batch-0008.yml`. **Code:**
  `batches/0008/remove_layers.py` (1,291 bytes), `census.py` (2,020).
  **Depends on:** batch 0004's authority, prompts and judge; batch 0005's
  fragment corpus and quantization cell; batch 0006's influence profile of
  Qwen3-0.6B and its weight-graph census of TriLM 390M; batch 0007's table
  and its floor.

## Hypotheses

- **H-S′, selection by signal.** Batch 0007 showed that no partition of
  Qwen3-1.7B's tensors by class tolerates Q4_0 at ε = 0, and that losses
  are superadditive. H-S says the parts that carry the fragment are
  measurable; H-S′ says they are selected by a signal rather than by a
  class: a map whose selection signal is the fragment itself keeps more
  of the fragment than the same map with an unrelated signal or none.
  The map is llama.cpp's own: `llama-imatrix` computes an importance
  matrix from a corpus and `llama-quantize --imatrix` protects the
  weights that corpus activates. Transformation T(signal): Q4_0 or
  Q4_K_M on every linear weight (embedding and output at float16) with
  the importance matrix from the signal corpus. Signals: the fragment's
  own 804 sentences (batch 0005's corpus) against the AGPL licence text
  from this repository, of comparable size.
- **H-G, approximate.** Removing layers of low influence (batch 0006:
  the mean change of the residual over the forward prompts) costs fewer
  canonical answers than removing layers of high influence.

## Derived predictions, with severity

- **P1 (H-S′), high severity.** Q4_0 with the fragment signal keeps more
  canonical answers than Q4_0 with the licence signal, by more than the
  floor (2): the signal selects. Refuted if the two are within 2, or the
  licence signal keeps more.
- **P2 (H-S′), high severity.** Q4_0 with the fragment signal keeps more
  canonical answers than Q4_0 without a signal (batch 0005: 91 to 92),
  by more than 10. Refuted if not. A pass says selection by signal does
  what partition by class could not; the size of the gain is the
  measurement.
- **P3 (H-S′), moderate severity.** Q4_K_M with the fragment signal keeps
  at least as many canonical answers as Q4_K_M without one, and both keep
  more than Q4_0 without one, because Q4_K_M's super-block scales are a
  finer map. Refuted if either inequality fails.
- **P4 (H-G), moderate severity.** For Qwen3-0.6B (baseline 79 canonical,
  batch 0004), removing layer 13 alone costs fewer than 5 canonical
  answers; removing 12, 13, 23 and 25 costs fewer than removing 2 and 3.
  Refuted if either fails. The influence metric is coarse (short prompts,
  all positions); a refutation lands on it first.
- **Replicates (no prediction).** Cells 01 to 03 repeat batch 0007's
  embedding, q/k and v/o cells. They give the floor for single-class Q4_0
  and decide whether −4, −2 and −3 were effects.
- **Census (description).** For TriLM 390M, TriLM 1.1B and
  SmolLM2-360M-Instruct: dead query/key dimensions per head and whole dead
  heads, dead value dimensions, dead and exactly duplicate FFN neurons,
  and every two-dimensional tensor's distinct values when at most eight
  (TriLM 1.1B's five values, open since batch 0005). The exact contraction
  of H-G is expressible in the architecture only for whole heads and for
  FFN neurons; the census says whether any instance exists.

## Auxiliaries

- llama.cpp's importance-matrix implementation and its Q4_0 and Q4_K
  kernels; the chunk length (512) of the signal pass; the floor of one to
  two countries for Q4_0 readouts.
- The prefix criterion; the mixed runner fleet.
- For the layers: `transformers`' handling of removed layers (the
  attention `layer_idx` is renumbered; the cache is not used).

## Rivals to exclude

- A fragment-signal gain could come from the signal's *form* (short
  declarative sentences) rather than its content; the licence control
  shares neither, so a later control with sentences of the same form and
  other content (another relation) would separate the two. Not in this
  batch; stated now.
- A layer-removal cost could come from renumbering rather than from the
  removal; the all-layers cell through the same code is the control.

**Census (cell 12).** TriLM 390M: only layer 0 has dead structure, 349
dead query or key dimensions spread over 16 heads (up to 59 per head), no
wholly dead head; no dead value dimensions, FFN neurons or duplicate
neurons. TriLM 1.1B: only layer 0, 1,506 dead query or key dimensions and
20 of its 28 heads wholly dead in query or key; every linear tensor holds
five values, two magnitudes besides zero per tensor (layer 0 q, k, v:
±0.00791 and ±0.00987; o: ±0.0318 and ±0.0329); the five values of batch
0005 are two magnitudes per tensor. SmolLM2-360M-Instruct: no exact
structure. A head whose query or key rows are zero has all-zero scores,
so softmax weights are uniform and the head outputs the running mean of
its values: exact, but a replacement of the head by a prefix average, not
a deletion (which corrects batch 0006's wording). Only a dimension dead in
both query and key of a rotary pair can be dropped outright; not yet
checked.

### The predictions

- **P1 refuted.** Fragment signal 132, licence signal 130: within the
  floor of 2. What the matrix protects does not depend on which of these
  two corpora produced it.
- **P2 met as stated, and the rival is stronger than the hypothesis.** 132
  against 91 to 92 is a gain of 40, more than 10. But the licence signal
  gives 130: the gain comes from using an importance matrix at all, not
  from the fragment being the signal. H-S′ in its stated form (the signal
  selects the fragment) is not supported; a generic importance matrix does
  most of the work.
- **P3 refuted.** Q4_K_M with the fragment signal (139) keeps fewer
  canonical answers than without (148); both keep more than Q4_0 without
  a signal. The fragment signal costs 9 here, above any floor measured.
- **P4 refuted in its first part.** Removing layer 13, the lowest-influence
  layer of batch 0006's profile, costs 15 canonical answers (79 to 64),
  not fewer than 5 and not benign; its collapse rises. The second part
  holds (removing 12, 13, 23, 25 costs 72, removing 2 and 3 costs 77) but
  both are destruction. The influence metric does not identify safe
  removals for this fragment; a refutation lands on it first, as stated
  beforehand.

### Findings beyond the predictions (post-designated)

- **llama.cpp's own recipe is a per-part map that fits the measured
  sensitivity.** Q4_K_M without any signal keeps 148 canonical answers
  (float16: 145) at 51 % of the size. It stores `attn_v` and `ffn_down` of
  14 of the 28 layers at Q6_K and the rest at Q4_K: the value path and the
  FFN down-projection, the classes batch 0007 found most sensitive
  under Q4_0 (down −15, v/o −3). Against batch 0007's criterion it keeps
  canonical (+3) and misses the strict form by one count on each of
  historical glut (3 against 2 for another's capital) and collapse (11
  against 10); the floor of Q4_K_M readouts is unmeasured. This is the
  nearest thing so far to a structure-preserving lossy map at substantial
  compression, found in a library, and was not predicted.
- **A generic importance matrix does most of what Q4_0 needed.** 91 to 92
  to 130 to 132.
- **PyTorch and llama.cpp give identical fragment counts** on Qwen3-0.6B
  at float.

### Auxiliaries and rivals

- The importance-matrix gain could come from calibration text being
  English prose at all; the licence control cannot exclude "any prose".
  A signal of the same form on another relation, and random text, would.
- Q4_K_M's recipe depends on llama.cpp's version (recorded in the cell).
- The layer metric: short prompts, all positions.
