  this document. The instance is defined here exactly, so that it exists before anything measures it.
- **Kind:** a construction (the instance, its carriers, its exact tables, its decompilation) with predictions and
  controls; and the first test of batch 0029's structural bounds at lengths above 1 against exact extrema.
- **Status:** run and graded. Six CI runs (38980018769 to 38981680715); the first five each stopped on one fault
  of the instrument (a tokenizer converter, an assertion of mine, an Oxigraph dump argument, three llama.cpp
  flags), none on the instance. Final run 38981680715: Q1, Q2, Q3, Q4, Q5 and controls a, b, c all as predicted.
  Results below.
- **Branch:** `batch/0030-tiny-complete`, on top of `batch/0029-feasibility` (`002e59c`).
  **Workflow:** `.github/workflows/batch-0030.yml`.
- **Depends on:** batch 0029 (`structural.py`, the taps, the exact-table method), batch 0014 (the pinned llama.cpp

No LARQL code, text or data is used in this batch.

## The instance, defined

The same open term as `HuggingFaceTB/SmolLM2-135M` (a pre-norm decoder: token embedding; blocks of RMSNorm,
grouped-query softmax attention with RoPE, residual add, RMSNorm, SiLU-gated feed-forward, residual add; final
RMSNorm; tied unembedding), with the sizes

| hidden | blocks | query heads | kv heads | head size | FFN | vocabulary | RMSNorm ε | RoPE θ | context |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 16 | 3 | 4 | 2 | 4 | 24 | 50 | 1e-5 | 100,000 | 64 |

and the substitution produced by SmolLM2's own published initialisation rule: every linear and embedding weight
drawn from a normal distribution with standard deviation 1/24 = 0.041666… (`config_smollm2_135M.yaml`,
`init_method.std`), every norm gain 1, under `torch.manual_seed(8)` (SmolLM2's seed; the stream is PyTorch's,
not nanotron's, so this is a seed-8 initialisation in the same rule, not SmolLM2's own w₀). Nothing is trained.
In the terms of the earlier discussion: this is a transformer decoder and a point w₀ of the training recurrence,
and whether it is a language model of anything is what the batch measures rather than assumes.

The vocabulary is a character alphabet of 50 symbols: `<unk>`, `<s>`, `</s>`, `<pad>`, space, newline, the 26
lowercase letters, the 10 digits, and `. , ? ! : - ' ( )`, held as a SentencePiece character model so that the
standard loaders and converters that read SmolLM2 read this instance by the same paths. A chat template of the
form `<s>user: … \nassistant: ` is attached.

## Cells

- **01 Build and carry.** The instance as a Hugging Face directory (`config.json`, `model.safetensors`,
  `tokenizer.model`, `tokenizer_config.json`, `generation_config.json`), hashed; its ONNX export by Optimum; its
  GGUF by llama.cpp's converter at `b11450`; the identity of the tensors across the three (names, shapes, dtypes,
  and the safetensors bytes against the GGUF payload hashes).
- **02 Slot in.** The same calls that run SmolLM2-135M: `transformers` `generate` through the chat template;
  ONNX Runtime on the export; llama.cpp (`llama-cli` or `llama-simple`) on the GGUF; logits of the three legs
  compared on the same prompts.
- **03 Exact tables, lengths 1 to 4.** Every input sequence over the 50 symbols: 50, 2,500, 125,000 and
  6,250,000 sequences, every next-token distribution computed. Per length: finiteness, the number of distinct
  logit rows, the number of distinct argmax values, the SHA-256 of the whole table in lexicographic order of the
  inputs, entropy and top-probability statistics, and the exact coordinate-wise extrema of every tapped activation
  (the taps of batch 0029). Rows are kept in full for lengths 1 and 2, summarised for 3 and 4.
- **04 The bounds at every length.** Batch 0029's `structural.py` on the instance; containment of the exact
  extrema of cell 03 at lengths 1, 2, 3 and 4.
- **05 Decompile to the end.** Every unit of every block (24 × 3) with its key readout (the gate row and the up
  row through the embedding, a score per symbol) and its value readout (the down column through the unembedding,
  a score per symbol); every head with its QK score table over the 50 symbols at relative offsets 0 to 3 (RoPE
  included) and its OV readout; written as ground N-Triples, with the tensor bytes referenced by SHA-256. Checks:
  no blank node (`ASK`), the triple count, the round trip of the byte references, the size of the RDF against the
  size of the safetensors file.
- **06 Working, against a fixed language.** L₁ = English text: `llama-perplexity` on a 16 KB public English
  sample restricted to the alphabet, reported in bits per character against the uniform floor log₂ 50 = 5.644.
  L₂ = the capital relation of batch 0004: the mass on the correct first character after "the capital of X is "
  for the 12 countries of batch 0004's calibration set, against 1/50. Rejection rule for trivial models, stated
  relative to the vocabulary: the length-1 argmax takes at least V/8 ≈ 6 distinct values and the length-1 rows
  are all distinct; this instance's status under the rule is recorded.
- **Controls.** (a) One unit in the last place of one norm gain: the table hashes change, the argmax of length 1
  changes in no row. (b) One weight set to NaN: cell 03 reports non-finite. (c) Layer 0's down-projection scaled
  by 2⁸: block 1's FFN box scales by exactly 2⁸.

## Predictions, with severity

- **Q1, moderate.** All three carriers are produced and all three harnesses run the instance from the same
  files that run SmolLM2: Optimum exports, ONNX Runtime's logits agree with PyTorch within 1e-4, llama.cpp's
  converter accepts the directory and llama.cpp's greedy ids on three prompts equal PyTorch's. Refuted per leg; a
  refutation names the tool and its message.
- **Q2, high.** The exact tables are finite at every length, and batch 0029's structural bounds contain every
  exact extremum at lengths 1, 2, 3 and 4. Refuted by one coordinate outside; then the length-independence
  argument of addendum 1 is wrong.
- **Q3, moderate.** The instance is not a working model of L₁: bits per character on the English sample within
  0.5 of the uniform floor (between 5.14 and 6.14). Refuted by a value below 5.14 (which would mean an
  initialisation already compresses English) or above 6.14 (worse than uniform by more than the margin, which
  would mean the measurement or the tokenizer path is wrong). On L₂: mass on the correct character within a
  factor of 3 of 1/50 for at least 10 of the 12 countries.
- **Q4, moderate.** The length-1 table has 50 distinct rows and at least 6 distinct argmax values (the instance
  passes the vocabulary-relative rejection rule for degenerate models even though it fails the working test),
  which shows the rejection rule and the working test are different conditions.
- **Q5, low.** The decompiled RDF is ground, and its byte-reference round trip is exact; the RDF is larger than
  the safetensors file (the instance is 30 KB, so the overhead of names dominates), with the ratio recorded.
- **Controls (a), (b), (c), high.** As stated; a refutation of (a)'s second clause or of (b) voids the
  corresponding readout.

## Observed, no prediction

The distinct-row and distinct-argmax counts at lengths 2 to 4; the looseness of the structural bounds per block
and per length; the three harnesses' outputs on the chat prompt, verbatim; the time and memory of each cell.

## What is not in this batch

Training steps of any kind; any claim about SmolLM2-135M's own w₀ (the seed stream differs); lengths above 4.

## Results (read from the logs and artifact of run 38981680715)

**The instance.** 6,672 parameters in 29 stored tensors (30 with the tied head), `model.safetensors` SHA-256
`d8259a3e830695deda860cc2f97634504f709ee5f027b1e3ddded4245ddb17eb`. Amendment to the registration, made before
the first run: no newline in the alphabet (SentencePiece normalises it); 4 specials + 26 letters + 10 digits + 9
marks + space = 50 pieces. The SentencePiece reading and the fast `tokenizer.json` reading of the same 50 pieces
differ by a leading `▁` (the slow reader prepends one); llama.cpp's own tokenizer prepends `<s>` and `▁`. The
same bytes, three tokenizations; every comparison below is made on the ids each harness actually used.

**Cell 01, carriers.** Optimum exports the ONNX graph (859 nodes, opset 18, inputs `input_ids`, `attention_mask`,
`position_ids`); llama.cpp's converter at `b11450` writes the GGUF (29 tensors). Byte identity with safetensors:
GGUF 23 of 29, the six exceptions being every q and k projection, which the converter permutes for its rotary
layout; ONNX 2 of 29, since the exporter transposes and fuses. Q1's carrier clauses held.

**Cell 02, slotted in.** `transformers` runs it through the chat template: `<s>user: the capital of france is
assistant: ` → 32 spaces (token 4, `▁`, 32 times). ONNX Runtime's logits agree with PyTorch to 1.2 × 10⁻⁷.
llama.cpp's greedy continuations of three prompts (`ssssssssssssssss`, `????????????????`, `6666666666666666`)
equal PyTorch's from the same ids. Q1 held in full.

**Cell 03, exact tables.** Every sequence over the 50 symbols to length 4:

| length | sequences | distinct rows | distinct argmax | mean entropy (bits) | residual \|max\| | logit \|max\| | time |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 50 | 50 | 33 | 5.623 | 0.226 | 0.699 | 0.0 s |
| 2 | 2,500 | 2,500 | 50 | 5.622 | 0.250 | 0.894 | 0.2 s |
| 3 | 125,000 | 125,000 | 50 | 5.621 | 0.256 | 0.942 | 1.3 s |
| 4 | 6,250,000 | 6,250,000 | 50 | 5.620 | 0.267 | 0.956 | 76.5 s |

All finite; every row distinct at every length (the length-4 table's SHA-256 is
`7a2e711beb0d90a9a34c4af1b0b4a9edb9a0d1dc7615da039d418d99ef838e23`). Q4 held: the instance passes the
vocabulary-relative rejection rule (50 distinct rows, 33 argmax values at length 1).

**Cell 04, the bounds at every length.** Batch 0029's structural bounds contain every exact extremum at lengths
1, 2, 3 and 4, maximum violation 0 (Q2 held; the first check of addendum 1's length-independence against exact
data above length 1). Bounds: residual after block 3 below 2.175 for every length, logits below 0.962; looseness
at the last residual 9.2, 8.3, 8.1, 7.8 for lengths 1 to 4, at the logits 1.14, 1.03, 1.01, 1.006 (the exact
length-4 logits come within 0.6 % of the bound).

**Cell 05, decompiled to the end.** 161,297 ground triples: 29 tensors by name, shape, dtype, bytes and
SHA-256; 50 symbols; 72 units with gate-key, up-key and value readouts over the 50 symbols; 12 heads with QK
tables at offsets 0 to 3 and OV readouts. Oxigraph 0.5.11 loads them, `ASK` for a blank node is false, every
tensor SHA-256 in the graph equals the bytes in the file, and the count is unchanged through a dump and reload.
N-Triples 26,980,267 bytes against a 29,600-byte safetensors file: ratio 911 (Q5 held, with the overhead of
names as predicted; at this size the triples are almost entirely IRIs).

**Cell 06, working.** L₁: 5.656 bits per character under PyTorch and 5.639 under `llama-perplexity` on the
16,384-character English sample, against the uniform floor 5.644. L₂: mass on the correct first character of
the capital between 0.77 and 0.99 times 1/50 for all twelve countries, argmax a space in every case. Q3 held:
the instance runs in every harness, answers every prompt, and is not a language model of English or of the
capital relation, by measurement.

**Controls.** (a) the null variant changes the length-1 table hash and no argmax; (b) the NaN copy is reported
non-finite; (c) block 1's FFN box scales by exactly 2⁸ and no other block moves.

**What the batch establishes.** For this instance the characterization is complete in the strict sense up to
length 4 in the language of outputs and tapped activations, sound for every length beyond by the structural
bounds, exact and ground at the content layer with a round trip, and decided on behaviour against two fixed
languages. It is the same open term, the same carriers, the same harnesses and the same instruments as
SmolLM2-135M, and the only difference between "a transformer decoder" and "a language model" that the batch
could measure is the behavioural one: this instance is the former and not the latter, and it passes every
structural test that SmolLM2 passes.
