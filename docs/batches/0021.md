## Purpose

Batch 0019 computed by a Datalog program, from the reified architecture and a hand-written library of groups, which class of transformation each gauge induces on each state of the forward pass, and measured the table on small models and on SmolLM2-135M. Batch 0020 proved, one operation at a time and for every nonlinearity, the lemmas of the library that the derivation relies on, and said plainly that the gluing of the lemmas into the statement about a whole attention block, a whole gated block and the whole model was not done. This batch does the gluing.

The question that motivates it is which objects of the forward pass are fixed by the symmetries of the weights: the residual stream, the arrays of the attention (queries `qv`, keys `kv`, values `vv`; a key/value cache keeps the last two), both or neither. The table below is what the theorems of this batch state, for every nonlinearity, in the model defined in `Model.lean`. Each theorem is an equality: either the array is equal to the original, or it is equal to an explicit formula applied to the original (`Q` applied to the stream; `A g` applied to the values; the complex scalar applied to the keys; indices permuted).

| | `qv`, `kv`, `vv` equal | some of `qv`, `kv`, `vv` given by a formula |
|---|---|---|
| **stream equal** | the norm weights (N1: `table_attnNorm`, `table_mlpNorm`) and the units of the feed-forward block (M1: `table_mlpScale`, `table_unitPerm`) | the value/output gauge (A1: values `A g` times the original; `table_ov`), the rotary-plane scalars (A3: keys the scalar times the original, queries its inverse transpose times the original; `table_qk`), the permutations of heads and groups (A6; `table_head`) |
| **stream given by a formula** (`Q` applied) | the gauges of the hidden coordinates (H1: signed permutations, with the norm weights permuted; H4 for constant norm weights: orthogonal matrices; `table_stream`, `logits_signedPerm`, `logits_orthogonal`) | a composition of a gauge of the hidden coordinates and the value/output gauge (`both_moved`) |

"Equal" for the stream means that a layer maps the same input to the same output; by induction over the layers the stream after every layer is the same. "Equal" for the arrays means equal at every position, group, plane. The formulas hold for every admissible parameter, the identity gauge included; the theorems do not say that an array differs from the original for a non-identity gauge. That it does is shown by measurement: batch 0015 on the cache of SmolLM2-135M-Instruct (the cache moved by 0.8 to 196 times its largest entry under A6, A1, A3), batch 0019 on all the states of the same model, and the `F_p` run of batch 0022 on one small instance.

What follows from the table is a statement about quantities computed from one state. Any function of the arrays that are equal is invariant under the gauges of the first column; any function of the stream alone is invariant under all gauges except H1 and H4. The logits are invariant under all of them. By the measurements, neither the stream nor the arrays are invariant under the whole group; the arrays are the more stable of the two under the gauges of the hidden coordinates (which are the ones used to obfuscate or to reparametrize a checkpoint) and the less stable under the gauges inside the attention block.

## The model

`Model.lean` defines a Llama-style decoder over an arbitrary field `K`: a vocabulary `V`; hidden dimension `d`; heads `Hh` and key/value groups `Gr` (arbitrary finite types) with a map of each head to its group; the head dimension of the queries and keys as a finite type `P` of rotary planes with a `2 × 2` block each; the rotation of every position and plane; the value dimension; the units of the gated block. The three functions that make it nonlinear are fields of the environment and are *variables*: the exponential of the attention (`expo`), the gate (`silu`) and the scale of the normalization (`rs`); nothing is said about their values. The attention is causal (the weight of a position after the query's is zero) and normalized; the normalization of the stream is `rms`, the function of batch 0020, with an arbitrary scale; the norm weights are kept (not folded). The arrays of a layer that the theorems track are `qv` (queries), `kv` (keys), `vv` (values), the first two after the rotation.

The correspondence with `model.py` (the flattened layout of Hugging Face's Llama, with the queries of all heads in one matrix, the pairs of the rotary embedding at `(i, i + head_dim/2)`) is by reading. It is tested in exact arithmetic by a later batch, not here.

## Registered claims

`claims21.tsv` lists thirty-one statements by name. `Axioms21.lean` prints the axioms of seventy-one declarations of the new modules: the 31 statements; six lemmas that hold by `rfl` (`qv_ov`, `kv_ov`, `vv_qk`, `qv_head`, `kv_head`, `vv_head`: an array does not read the fields that the gauge changes, or the gauge only re-indexes the definition; they follow from the definitions and are not claims); and 34 helper lemmas (the congruence lemmas of `Glue.lean`, `submatrix_mulVec_row`, `score_of_qk`, `sp_mulVec`, ...). The cache parts of `table_mlpScale`, `table_unitPerm`, `table_mlpNorm` and the `qv`, `kv` parts of `table_ov`, the three array parts of `table_head` and the `vv` part of `table_qk` are the `rfl` lemmas and nothing more. The honest content of the batch is the layer-function statements (`layerFn_*`, `logits_stream`, `logits_gauged` and the instances) and the formulas `vv_ov`, `kv_qk`, `qv_qk` and `*_stream`, `*_attnNorm`. `grade21.py` marks a claim accepted when a theorem of that name is listed and depends on no `sorryAx`; it does not compare the statement with the sentence of the claim.

| group | theorems | statement |
|---|---|---|
| F | `layerFn_ov`, `layerFn_qk`, `layerFn_headPerm`, `layerFn_mlpScale`, `layerFn_unitPerm`, `layerFn_attnNorm`, `layerFn_mlpNorm` | the function of a layer is unchanged by each of seven gauges, for every environment (nonlinearity, rotation of the complex form, scale) |
| S | `layerFn_stream`, `logits_stream`, `logits_signedPerm`, `logits_orthogonal` | for a dot-product-preserving `Q` with a left inverse, readers times `Qi`, writers `Q` times, embedding carried, head reading through `Qi`, compatible norm weights: a layer is equivariant and the logits of every model are unchanged; instances: every signed permutation (norm weights permuted) and every orthogonal matrix for models whose norm weights, including the final one, are constant vectors |
| C | `layerFn_gauged`, `logits_gauged` | any sequence of the seven gauges, chosen independently layer by layer, leaves the logits unchanged |
| K | `qv_stream`, `kv_stream`, `vv_stream`, `vv_ov`, `qv_qk`, `kv_qk`, `qv_attnNorm`, `kv_attnNorm`, `vv_attnNorm` | the equation that relates each array to the original, for the stream, value/output, query/key and attention-norm gauges |
| T | `table_stream`, `table_ov`, `table_qk`, `table_head`, `table_mlpScale`, `table_unitPerm`, `table_attnNorm`, `table_mlpNorm`, `both_moved` | the conjunction, for each gauge, of the statement about the layer and the equations about the arrays: the table above |

The hypotheses that the statements carry, and no others: `B g * A g = 1` (value/output); `a² + b² ≠ 0` for every plane scalar, and every rotation `E.rot t p` of the complex form `[[c, -s], [s, c]]` (query/key); `E.gr (σ hd) = τ (E.gr hd)` (heads and groups); `c i ≠ 0` (scalings); `PreservesDot Q`, a left inverse `Qi` and `Compat` (stream), where `Compat Q γ γ'` says `γ' ⊙ (Q r) = Q (γ ⊙ r)` for every `r` (so a constant weight is compatible with every `Q`, and a weight permuted by `σ` with the signed permutation of `σ`). `logits_orthogonal` therefore covers models whose norm weights, including the final norm weight, are constant; a trained checkpoint has non-constant weights, and composing the norm gauge (including the final norm) with H4 to reach such a model is not formalized.

## What this does not do

- It does not prove that these are *all* the gauges (the groups of batch 0019 are the largest ones). Batch 0015 counted the dimension numerically (seven configurations matched the formula) and batch 0019 computed it from a hand-written library of groups; neither is a proof of completeness.
- It does not prove anything about the real checkpoints: the theorems are about the Lean definition of the forward pass. That the Lean definition is the forward pass of `model.py` (and of Hugging Face's Llama, and of the other runtimes of batch 0014) is by reading, and is the subject of a later batch with exact arithmetic.
- It does not cover the gauges that are not symmetries (a general invertible matrix on the queries and keys, a scale on the gate branch, the centring of the embeddings of QuaRot and SpinQuant for models with RMSNorm), nor a model with LayerNorm, nor a rotary embedding that acts on a part of the head dimension.
- Division by zero is zero in Lean; the statements are equalities between terms built with the same division, so they hold for a zero attention normalizer as well, and say nothing about it.
- The rotation of the stream with the norm weights kept (H4t: the readers conjugated with the diagonal matrices of the weights) is not covered: `Compat` holds for a general orthogonal `Q` only for constant norm weights.
- The table says which arrays are equal and which are given by the explicit formula; it does not say that an array differs from the original for a given non-identity gauge (a witness for that is the measurement of batches 0015 and 0019).
- `logits_orthogonal` (H4) covers models with constant norm weights only.

**What this establishes.** For every field `K`, every function in the place of the exponential of the attention, the gate and the scale of the normalization, and every rotation of the complex form, in the Lean definition of a Llama-style decoder with causal softmax attention, grouped heads, rotary planes, a gated feed-forward block and norm weights that are kept:

- each of seven gauges (the value/output gauge, the complex scalars on the rotary planes, permutations of heads and groups, the up-branch scaling and the permutation of the units of the gated block, the two norm-weight scalings) leaves the function of a layer unchanged (`layerFn_ov`, `layerFn_qk`, `layerFn_headPerm`, `layerFn_mlpScale`, `layerFn_unitPerm`, `layerFn_attnNorm`, `layerFn_mlpNorm`), and so, applied in any order and with parameters chosen independently for every layer, leaves the logits of every model unchanged (`layerFn_gauged`, `logits_gauged`);
- the gauge of the residual stream by a matrix that preserves the dot product and has a left inverse, with the readers multiplied by the inverse and the writers by the matrix, the embedding carried and the head read through the inverse, makes every layer equivariant and leaves the logits unchanged (`layerFn_stream`, `logits_stream`) when the norm weights are compatible; every signed permutation with the norm weights permuted along (H1: `logits_signedPerm`) and every orthogonal matrix for models with constant norm weights (H4: `logits_orthogonal`) are instances;
- the arrays `qv`, `kv`, `vv` of a layer: the stream gauge leaves them equal (`qv_stream`, `kv_stream`, `vv_stream`); under the value/output gauge the values are `A g` times the original values (`vv_ov`); under the rotary-plane scalars the keys are the scalar times the original keys and the queries its inverse transpose times the original queries (`kv_qk`, `qv_qk`); the norm-weight scaling leaves all three equal (`qv_attnNorm`, `kv_attnNorm`, `vv_attnNorm`). That the value/output gauge leaves queries and keys equal, that the rotary-plane scalars leave the values equal, that the permutation of heads and groups only re-indexes the three arrays, and that the feed-forward gauges leave them equal, hold by `rfl` (the arrays do not read the fields that these gauges change);
- the table of the purpose section, as nine theorems (`table_stream` ... `both_moved`), each the conjunction of the layer statement and the equations about the arrays; `both_moved` states that for the composition of the stream gauge and the value/output gauge the output is `Q` applied to the original output, the values are `A g` times the original values and the keys are equal.

**What this says about the question that motivated the batch.** The equalities hold for the gauges of the hidden coordinates (permutations, signs, rotations, with norm weights permuted along or constant), the norm-weight scalings and the feed-forward gauges for `qv`, `kv`, `vv`; the stream is equal under the gauges inside the attention block (value/output, rotary scalars, permutations of heads and groups), the norm-weight scalings and the feed-forward gauges. If, as the measurements of batches 0015 and 0019 show on SmolLM2-135M-Instruct, the formulas change the arrays for non-identity gauges, then a fingerprint computed from `kv`, `vv` alone cannot tell two checkpoints apart when they differ by a gauge of the hidden coordinates, and one computed from the stream alone cannot when they differ by a gauge inside the attention block. The logits are invariant under all of them. That an array differs for a given non-identity gauge is not in the theorems.

**What it does not establish.** That these seven gauges and the stream gauge are all the symmetries (the completeness of the groups; batches 0015 and 0019 give the numerical and the computed count, no proof). That the Lean definition is the forward pass of `model.py`, of Hugging Face's Llama and of the runtimes of batch 0014 (batch 0022 compares the definition with `model.py` and `gauge.py` over `F_p` on small instances). Anything about the non-symmetries, LayerNorm, partial rotary embeddings, the rotation of the stream with norm weights kept (H4t), or a gauge of the final norm weight (the model-level scaling of `gf` with the head's columns is not formalized: batch 0015's `norm_gauge` applies it to the final norm, and the Lean statements do not); without it, `logits_orthogonal` covers models with constant norm weights only.
