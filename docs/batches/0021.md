## Purpose

Batch 0019 derived by a Datalog program, from the reified architecture, which class of transformation each gauge induces on each state of the forward pass, and measured the table on small models and on SmolLM2-135M (7 of 7 lines as predicted). Batch 0020 proved, one operation at a time and for every nonlinearity, the lemmas of the library that the derivation relies on, and said plainly that the gluing of the lemmas into the statement about a whole attention block, a whole gated block and the whole model was not done. This batch does the gluing.

The question that motivates it is which objects of the forward pass are fixed by the symmetries of the weights: the residual stream, the key/value cache, both or neither. The table below is the answer that the theorems of this batch state, for every nonlinearity, in the model defined in `Model.lean`.

| | cache fixed | cache moved |
|---|---|---|
| **stream fixed** | the norm weights (N1: `table_attnNorm`, `table_mlpNorm`) and the units of the feed-forward block (M1: `table_mlpScale`, `table_unitPerm`) | the value/output gauge (A1: values move by `A g`; `table_ov`), the rotary-plane scalars (A3: keys by the scalar, queries by its inverse transpose; `table_qk`), the permutations of heads and groups (A6; `table_head`) |
| **stream moved** | the gauges of the hidden coordinates (H1: signed permutations, with the norm weights permuted; H4: orthogonal matrices, with the norm weights folded; `table_stream`, `logits_signedPerm`, `logits_orthogonal`) | no single gauge; a composition of a gauge of the hidden coordinates and the value/output gauge moves both (`both_moved`) |

"Fixed" for the stream means that a layer maps the same input to the same output; by induction over the layers the stream after every layer is the same. "Fixed" for the cache means equal as arrays at every position, group, plane. "Moved" is the explicit formula of the theorem (`Q` applied to the stream; `A g` applied to the values; the complex scalar applied to the keys), which for a non-identity gauge changes the array; the batches 0015 and 0019 measured that it does on real models.

What follows from the table is a statement about quantities computed from one state. Any function of the cache alone is invariant under the gauges of the first column (the cache is literally equal), and not in general under those of the second; any function of the stream alone is invariant under all gauges except H1 and H4. The logits are invariant under all of them. So neither state is invariant by itself; the cache is the more stable of the two under the gauges of the hidden coordinates (which are the ones used to obfuscate or to reparametrize a checkpoint) and the less stable under the gauges inside the attention block.

## The model

`Model.lean` defines a Llama-style decoder over an arbitrary field `K`: a vocabulary `V`; hidden dimension `d`; heads `Hh` and key/value groups `Gr` (arbitrary finite types) with a map of each head to its group; the head dimension of the queries and keys as a finite type `P` of rotary planes with a `2 × 2` block each; the rotation of every position and plane; the value dimension; the units of the gated block. The three functions that make it nonlinear are fields of the environment and are *variables*: the exponential of the attention (`expo`), the gate (`silu`) and the scale of the normalization (`rs`); nothing is said about their values. The attention is causal (the weight of a position after the query's is zero) and normalized; the normalization of the stream is `rms`, the function of batch 0020, with an arbitrary scale; the norm weights are kept (not folded). The cache of a layer is the triple `qv` (queries), `kv` (keys), `vv` (values), the first two after the rotation.

The correspondence with `model.py` (the flattened layout of Hugging Face's Llama, with the queries of all heads in one matrix, the pairs of the rotary embedding at `(i, i + head_dim/2)`) is by reading. It is tested in exact arithmetic by a later batch, not here.

## Registered claims

`claims21.tsv` lists thirty-seven theorems by name; `Axioms21.lean` prints the axioms of all seventy-one theorems of the new modules, the thirty-four others being the steps that these use.

| group | theorems | statement |
|---|---|---|
| F | `layerFn_ov`, `layerFn_qk`, `layerFn_headPerm`, `layerFn_mlpScale`, `layerFn_unitPerm`, `layerFn_attnNorm`, `layerFn_mlpNorm` | the function of a layer is unchanged by each of seven gauges, for every environment (nonlinearity, rotation of the complex form, scale) |
| S | `layerFn_stream`, `logits_stream`, `logits_signedPerm`, `logits_orthogonal` | for a dot-product-preserving `Q` with a left inverse, readers times `Qi`, writers `Q` times, embedding carried, head reading through `Qi`, compatible norm weights: a layer is equivariant and the logits of every model are unchanged; instances: every signed permutation (norm weights permuted) and every orthogonal matrix (constant norm weights) |
| C | `layerFn_gauged`, `logits_gauged` | any sequence of the seven gauges, chosen independently layer by layer, leaves the logits unchanged |
| K | `qv_stream`, `kv_stream`, `vv_stream`, `qv_ov`, `kv_ov`, `vv_ov`, `qv_qk`, `kv_qk`, `vv_qk`, `qv_head`, `kv_head`, `vv_head`, `qv_attnNorm`, `kv_attnNorm`, `vv_attnNorm` | what each gauge does to each array of the cache, as an equation |
| T | `table_stream`, `table_ov`, `table_qk`, `table_head`, `table_mlpScale`, `table_unitPerm`, `table_attnNorm`, `table_mlpNorm`, `both_moved` | the conjunction, for each gauge, of what it does to the layer and to the cache: the table above |

The hypotheses that the statements carry, and no others: `B g * A g = 1` (value/output); `a² + b² ≠ 0` for every plane scalar, and every rotation `E.rot t p` of the complex form `[[c, -s], [s, c]]` (query/key); `E.gr (σ hd) = τ (E.gr hd)` (heads and groups); `c i ≠ 0` (scalings); `PreservesDot Q`, a left inverse `Qi` and `Compat` (stream), where `Compat Q γ γ'` says `γ' ⊙ (Q r) = Q (γ ⊙ r)` for every `r` (so a constant weight is compatible with every `Q`, and a weight permuted by `σ` with the signed permutation of `σ`).

## What this does not do

- It does not prove that these are *all* the gauges (the groups of batch 0019 are the largest ones). Batch 0015 counted the dimension numerically (seven configurations matched the formula) and batch 0019 derived it; neither is a proof of completeness.
- It does not prove anything about the real checkpoints: the theorems are about the Lean definition of the forward pass. That the Lean definition is the forward pass of `model.py` (and of Hugging Face's Llama, and of the other runtimes of batch 0014) is by reading, and is the subject of a later batch with exact arithmetic.
- It does not cover the gauges that are not symmetries (a general invertible matrix on the queries and keys, a scale on the gate branch, the centring of the embeddings of QuaRot and SpinQuant for models with RMSNorm), nor a model with LayerNorm, nor a rotary embedding that acts on a part of the head dimension.
- Division by zero is zero in Lean; the statements are equalities between terms built with the same division, so they hold for a zero attention normalizer as well, and say nothing about it.
- The rotation of the stream with the norm weights kept (H4t: the readers conjugated with the diagonal matrices of the weights) is not covered: `Compat` holds for a general orthogonal `Q` only for constant norm weights, which is H4 with the weights folded.
- The table says which arrays are equal and which are moved by the explicit formula; it does not say that a moved array differs from the original for a given non-identity gauge (a witness for that is the measurement of batches 0015 and 0019).
- The cross-check in the grader compares the table that the theorems state with the classes that batch 0019 measured; it was written with the 0019 table in view, so its agreement is a consistency check, not an independent prediction.
