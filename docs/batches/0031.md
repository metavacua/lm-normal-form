# Batch 0031: the smallest models of two stated theories, constructed in SmolLM2's term family and checked exhaustively

- **Registered:** 2026-10-17, before any cell ran. The constructions are stated here in closed form; the code is
  written after this document; nothing has been run.
- **Kind:** a construction with predictions, controls and a search (the width ladder).
- **Branch:** `batch/0031-minimal-models`, on top of `batch/0030-tiny-complete` (`ccc256b`).
  **Workflow:** `.github/workflows/batch-0031.yml`, not yet written.
- **Depends on:** batch 0030 (the carriers, harnesses, tokenizer construction and exact-table method), batch 0029
  (`structural.py`), the correspondence stated on 2026-10-16: a theory is a finite ground RDF graph, a model is a
  transformer under a fixed interpretation I, and M ⊨ T iff T ⊆ Th_I(M).

No LARQL code, text or data is used in this batch.

## Purpose

Minimality is relative to a theory. The smallest model of T under I is the least closed term with Th_I(M) ⊇ T,
and the exactly minimal one asserts T and nothing else. This batch constructs, rather than trains, the smallest
models of two theories inside SmolLM2-135M's term family (pre-norm RMSNorm decoder, grouped-query softmax
attention with RoPE, SiLU-gated FFN, tied head), checks soundness and completeness exhaustively on T's prompts,
records what the models assert outside T (over-generation), measures them under the information rules, and runs
them in the three harnesses so that "valid" means what it meant for SmolLM2 and for batch 0030's instance.

## The theories and their interpretations

**T₁ = {capital(france, paris)}**, one atom. Alphabet: the 50-piece character alphabet of batch 0030.
Interpretation I₁: the prompt "the capital of X is " (with the trailing space); the model asserts
capital(X, Y) when greedy decoding from the prompt emits the characters of Y followed by `</s>` or a space.
Soundness and completeness for T₁ are checked on the single prompt; the other eleven country prompts of T₂ are run
through the same I₁ and recorded as over-generation.

**T₂ = {capital(X, cap(X)) : X in the twelve countries of batch 0030}**: france→paris, germany→berlin,
italy→rome, spain→madrid, japan→tokyo, china→beijing, russia→moscow, egypt→cairo, canada→ottawa, brazil→brasilia,
india→delhi, australia→canberra. Alphabet: 50 word pieces: `<unk> <s> </s> <pad>`, `the capital of is`, the twelve
countries, the twelve capitals, and eighteen filler words. Interpretation I₂: the prompt `the capital of X is`
(five tokens); the model asserts capital(X, Y) when the greedy next token after the prompt is Y. Checked on the
twelve prompts; the other 38 values of X are recorded as over-generation.

## The constructions, in closed form

**M₁, zero blocks, for T₁.** With no blocks the model is the bilinear form E·diag(g_f)·Eᵀ after RMSNorm, so the
logit of y after x is e_x G e_y up to a positive factor, symmetric in x and y. The required chain
space→p→a→r→i→s→`</s>` is realised by a symmetric score matrix S with S[space,p]=1, S[p,a]=2, S[a,r]=3,
S[r,i]=4, S[i,s]=5, S[s,`</s>`]=6, zero elsewhere: each symbol's largest off-diagonal score is its successor's.
S = Q Λ Qᵀ; E = Q·|Λ|^{1/2} restricted to the non-zero eigenvalues, g_f = sign(Λ) on those coordinates (a signed
gain is an admissible weight of the norm sort). The width is d₁ = rank S ≤ 12; the exact value is observed. The
model ignores every prompt character but the last, so it answers "paris" to every prompt ending in a space.

**M₂, one block, one head, for T₂.** Zero blocks cannot be sound for T₂, since the twelve prompts share the last
token `is` and a zero-block model's output depends on the last token only. One block suffices:
- codes: the 50 tokens as unit vectors on coordinates 0–49 of a width-52 residual; coordinate 50 holds the same
  constant β for every token (the constant coordinate of the 10-16 derivation); coordinate 51 is unused;
- a previous-token head: W_q and W_k read only coordinate 50, so q and k do not depend on the token; both lie in
  rotary plane (0, 26) whose frequency is 1 radian per position, with k rotated by φ = 1 so that the score peaks
  at relative offset 1, and scaled so the softmax puts all but e⁻²⁰ of its mass on the previous position;
- W_v = identity on coordinates 0–49; W_o = α · Σ_X e_{cap(X)} e_Xᵀ with α = 5: the attention output at `is` is
  the code of X, and the o-projection writes α times the code of cap(X) into the residual;
- the FFN is inert: gate weights 0 (SiLU(0) = 0), so the block adds exactly the attention term;
- g_f = 1 on coordinates 0–49 and 0 on 50–51; the tied head then gives logit_t = n_last[t], which is α for cap(X),
  1 for `is`, and 0 otherwise.
Every step is exact in real arithmetic with orthogonal codes; the batch measures it in float32.

**The width ladder for M₂.** The same construction with codes that are not orthogonal: for d in 52, 40, 32, 24,
16, 12, 8, the 50 token codes are unit vectors drawn under seed 31, the constant coordinate is kept, W_o is the
least-squares (pseudoinverse) solution V K⁺ of the twelve associations, and the exhaustive check decides. The
smallest d that passes is the measured answer to "smallest" at this granularity for T₂ under I₂.

## Cells

- **01 Construct** M₁ and M₂ (and the ladder) as Hugging Face directories with their tokenizers (fast
  `tokenizer.json`; a SentencePiece `tokenizer.model` beside it for llama.cpp's converter), hashed; the parameter
  counts.
- **02 Check, exhaustively.** For each model and its theory: every prompt of T, the greedy assertion, soundness
  (every assertion in T) and completeness (every atom of T asserted); the margin (logit of the asserted token minus
  the runner-up); the assertions on every other value of X (over-generation); the information rules on the prompt
  domain: input dependence (mutual information between X and the next token under uniform X over the 50
  candidates) and context use (the same with the last token held fixed, which for I₂ is the whole quantity).
- **03 Slot in.** transformers greedy, ONNX export and ONNX Runtime logits, llama.cpp conversion and greedy on
  its own ids, as in batch 0030.
- **04 Bounds.** `structural.py` on M₂; containment of the exact extrema over the prompt domain.
- **Controls.** (a) W_o with two capitals swapped: soundness must fail on exactly those two subjects. (b) One
  weight set to NaN: the check reports non-finite. (c) The null variant: the hash changes, no assertion changes.

## Predictions, with severity

- **R1, high.** M₁ is sound and complete for T₁: greedy from "the capital of france is " emits p, a, r, i, s,
  `</s>`. Refuted by any other string. Observed with it: M₁ emits "paris" after all eleven other prompts.
- **R2, high.** d₁ = rank S is at most 12, and M₁ has fewer than 700 parameters (50·d₁ + d₁ gains). Refuted
  otherwise.
- **R3, high.** M₂ at width 52 is sound and complete for T₂ with margin at least 3 on every prompt, in float32.
  Refuted by one prompt.
- **R4, high.** M₂'s context use is above 3 bits on the prompt domain (twelve subjects, twelve distinct answers,
  uniform over X gives log₂ 12 = 3.58 bits of dependence through the previous token) and M₁'s is exactly 0.
  Refuted otherwise.
- **R5, moderate.** The ladder passes at d = 24 and fails at d = 8. Refuted if 24 fails or 8 passes.
- **R6, moderate.** transformers and ONNX Runtime run both models and agree within 1e-4; llama.cpp's converter
  accepts M₂ and refuses M₁ (zero blocks). Refuted per leg.
- **Controls (a), (b), (c), high**, as stated.

## Observed, no prediction

d₁; the margins; the over-generation tables; the sizes of M₂ on the ladder against |T₂| in bits (twelve
associations among twelve objects, log₂ 12! ≈ 28.8 bits, against parameters × 32 bits); the time of each cell.

## What is not in this batch

Training; any theory with more than one relation; prompts outside the two templates; a proof that the ladder's
smallest passing width is minimal over all codes (it is minimal over the codes tried, and the registration says so).
