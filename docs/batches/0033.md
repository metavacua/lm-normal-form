## Purpose

For a fact a (a prompt and a target token) the margin m_a(σ) is the logit of the target minus the largest other
logit; the model is sound for a when m_a > 0. Soundness for a set of facts is one strict inequality per fact over the
same weights. The weights that appear in m_a are the path of a, and the algebraic expression of m_a through them can
be differentiated exactly. This batch examines that algebra and uses it to choose substitute weights at three
granularities:

1. **Element-wise**: one weight, one fact. The margin is a function of one number.
2. **Sequent-wise**: one fact (one input, one output), the set S of weights on its path.
3. **Graph-wise**: several facts whose paths intersect; shared weights carry the constraints of every fact through
   them, and the facts the model currently gets right enter as constraints to preserve.

## The algebra, for the exact cases

Let x be the residual at the last position after the last layer, n = x / √(mean(x²) + ε_n) its normalisation, g the
final-norm gain, E the tied embedding (also the head), z = E (g ⊙ n) the logits, and a the last position's
feed-forward activation vector before the last layer's down projection. Three kinds of weight enter the logits
without passing through any further nonlinearity beyond the final norm, so the margin as a function of that weight
is exact in closed form:

- the final-norm gain g_i: z′ = z + (g′_i − g_i) n_i E[:, i], affine in g_i;
- a head-row entry E[v, i] (output side; exact when token v is not in the prompt): only z_v changes,
  z′_v = z_v + (E′[v, i] − E[v, i]) g_i n_i, affine in the entry;
- a last-layer down-projection entry W[i, j]: x′ = x + (W′_ij − W_ij) a_j e_i, then z′ = E (g ⊙ rms(x′)), a ratio of an
  affine function and the square root of a quadratic in W′_ij.

For these the margin is evaluated at every finite bf16 value of the weight (65,280 values), so the feasible set
{w′ : m_a(w′) ≥ ε} is computed exactly and not estimated. For every other weight the margin is a composition through
attention, SiLU and normalisations; it is evaluated on a 513-point grid by full forward passes and its derivative is
exact by autograd.

- **01 The path of France (the base model).** The exact gradient of France's margin with respect to every weight.
  The path size (weights with non-zero gradient), the concentration of first-order attribution |g·w| by tensor
  sort and by layer, the top coordinates. Finite-difference check of the gradient on 12 weights (4 per exact kind).
- **02 Element-wise.** The 10 largest-|g| weights of each exact kind (30) enumerated over all finite bf16 values,

  effect on all 189 facts is measured. The enumeration is checked against full forward passes at the current value
  and at 3 other values per weight.
- **03 Sequent-wise (France).** Support S = the 30,000 largest-|g| coordinates. (a) minimum L2 change:
  Δ = (ε − m)·g / ‖g‖², repeated with the gradient re-taken at the rounded weights (Newton), up to 8 rounds.
  (b) minimum L1 change with a box |Δ_i| ≤ max(0.25|w_i|, 0.01): a fractional knapsack, solved exactly by taking
  coordinates in decreasing |g_i|. Reported: rounds to reach margin ≥ ε, the number of weights whose bf16 value
  changed, ‖Δ‖₂, ‖Δ‖₁, ‖Δ‖₂/‖w_S‖₂, the effect on the other 188 facts, the effect on bits per token over a fixed
  English sample (SmolLM2's tokenizer, 4,096 tokens).
- **04 Graph-wise.** Support S = the 30,000 largest max-over-constraint-facts |g| coordinates. A linear program
  (HiGHS) per round: minimise ‖Δ‖₁ + 100·Σ slack subject to, for every install fact, m + JΔ + slack ≥ ε; for every
  currently-correct fact outside the install set, m + JΔ + slack ≥ min(ε, m); the box |Δ_i| ≤ trust·max(0.25|w_i|, 0.01);
  J the exact Jacobian at the current rounded weights. Slack above 1e-6 names a fact in conflict. Up to 6 rounds, the
  trust factor 1, 1, 0.5, 0.5, 0.25, 0.25, re-linearised each round, stopped when every install fact is correct.
  Runs: **G11** (install the 11 of the twelve countries of batches 0030 to 0032 that the authority labels; preserve
  the currently correct facts outside them), **G189** (install every currently wrong fact; preserve the currently
  correct ones), **G11perm** (install a cyclic shift of the 11 targets; same preserve set).
- **Controls.** (a) G11perm against G11. (b) The gradient finite-difference check. (c) A random change with the
  same support and the same L2 norm as France's sequent-wise substitute.

## Amendment 1, 2026-10-17, before any run: verifying the variables comes first

The first part of the experiment is to verify that the isolated variables are the correct ones. Cell 0V runs first and
gates the rest: if V1 to V4 do not hold, nothing in cells 02 to 04 is read as a finding, only as a measurement with an
unverified premise. Four facts shape what "correct variable" can mean here.

- Nearly every weight has a non-zero gradient: attention weights are shared across positions, the head touches the
  whole vocabulary, no activation is exactly zero. The path in the structural sense is therefore almost the whole model,
  and "isolating the path" is a selection by effect, not a cut by connectivity. The one genuinely off-path set is
  the rows of the tied embedding for tokens that are neither the target, nor the current runner-up, nor in the prompt:
  their gradient is exactly zero and their effect is exactly zero so long as their logit stays below the runner-up.
  The margin is a maximum, so off-path is conditional on that threshold. That is a finding about the variable set: it
  includes the identity of the current competitor.
- The margin decomposes exactly into the embedding term and the attention and feed-forward deltas of every layer,
  because the final normalisation divides by a scalar fixed once the residual is fixed. That decomposition is what
  LARQL's `TRACE ... FOR ... DECOMPOSE` reports. Cell 0V computes it independently, from hooks, so the autograd
  isolation and the decomposition can be compared with each other.
- For three kinds of weight the derivative has a closed form (the final-norm gain, the head row of the target and the
  competitor, the last layer's down projection), derived by hand in this document's previous section. The autograd
  value must equal the closed form on every entry.
- First order is a claim about small changes. Whether the coordinates with the largest |g·h| are the ones whose
  finite changes move the margin most is a measurement, made on a sample of coordinates by actual forward passes.

## Amendment 3, 2026-10-17, after the substitution run was cancelled: the task is the isolation of the path

The substitution cells (element-wise, sequence-wise, graph-wise) were written and a run of them was started and
cancelled before any of them finished (run 39050822304). They solved for replacement weights, which is a later step.
The task of this batch is to isolate the weights and the IO path correctly. The code for the cancelled cells stays in
`batches/0033/` (`element.py`, `substitute.py`, `grade33.py`'s T-lines) and is not run by this batch.

**What the path is.** For one input and one output the path is the structure of the computation, identifiable
without reference to what values the weights take: the embedding rows of the prompt tokens, the attention heads of
each layer and the source positions each reads, the feed-forward units of each layer and position that carry the
signal, the final norm, and the head rows of the target and the competitor. A weight on the path is named by an
address that holds in any model of the family (role, relative depth, index) and, within one model, by its tensor,
row and column in each encoding (the records already give those). Selection by gradient size, which cell 0V verified
as a measure of effect, is not the path; it is one score among several that can rank its members.

**The path as a graph.** Nodes: the embedding, each attention head, each feed-forward unit, the final norm, the head.
Edges: the residual stream's additions, with an exact weight on each edge: the contribution of an earlier node's
output to a later unit's gate and up pre-activations, which is linear in that output once the normalisation scalar is
fixed. The edges therefore decompose each unit's pre-activation exactly into the contributions of the nodes that feed it.

**Verifying an isolation.** An isolated set is correct if it is sufficient and not larger than needed. Sufficiency:
the forward pass restricted to the set (every other feed-forward unit and head masked) reproduces the model's output
for that input. Parsimony: the smallest set at each criterion that does so, compared with a random set of the same
size as a control. The criteria compared: the unit's activation magnitude, its gate pre-activation (the criterion of
LARQL's walk, a nearest-neighbour search over gate rows), its direct contribution to the margin (last position
only), and random.

**Cell 06.** For SmolLM2-135M, 135M-Instruct and SmolLM2-360M (32 layers, hidden 960, 15 query and 5 key-value heads,

## Amendment 4, 2026-10-17: the isolation is a trace, not an ablation

Cell 06 masked units and heads and asked whether the output survived. That writes to the computation, answers a
different question (sufficiency under a selector) and is not the isolation asked for. The isolation is non-destructive:
run the single prompt once, observe exactly what executes, and map what was observed onto the model's own structures.
Nothing is zeroed, masked, patched, rescaled or pruned. Pruning the DAG, ablation, substitution and the sufficiency
curves of cell 06 are deferred until this isolation has been checked.

**What is observed, with standard tools.**
- The executed operations, in order, each with the module that issued it, the parameter tensors it read and how many
  elements of each: PyTorch's `TorchDispatchMode` (the dispatcher's own hook for every operator that runs) together
  with `torch.utils.module_tracker.ModuleTracker` (the module that is executing). The record is the DAG of what ran:
  an operation's inputs are produced by earlier operations or are parameters, so the order is a topological order.
- The structural cone of the output, by automatic differentiation of the margin with respect to every intermediate
  tensor and every attention probability: an element whose gradient is exactly zero cannot influence the output and is
  not on the path; an element with a non-zero gradient can. This is the dependency structure of the executed graph,
  read, not perturbed.
- The exact flow: the residual stream is a sum of node outputs, so the margin is exactly the sum of each node's direct
  contribution; each attention head's output is exactly the sum over source positions of the attention probability
  times the value, so each head's contribution splits exactly by the token it read; each feed-forward unit's
  contribution is exactly its coefficient times a column; each unit's gate and up pre-activations split exactly
  by the nodes that feed them.

**Checks without ablation.** Conservation: every decomposition sums to the quantity it decomposes. Coverage: the set of
parameters the trace says were read is compared with the parameter list of the model. Causality: no gradient flows from a
later position to an earlier one. Structure: in the last layer only the last position is in the cone.

## Amendment 6, 2026-10-17: the control is the model's own unsoundness, and the characterisation is differential

The base model's wrong answers are used as a control, not corrected. The naive assumption that every weight is responsible for
the output is impeached by the wrong outcome; the constructive replacement is to isolate which weights and paths are more
responsible for the wrong outcome than others. A feature is a feature (an addressable gate row and down column) whatever it does
on a given prompt. The structural similarity of other models is the instrument: the base model's own pre-training checkpoints (same
architecture and coordinates, every 240,000 steps up to 1,920,000, then the final model) and SmolLM2-135M-Instruct (fine-tuned from
it) share every weight address with it. For two such models A and B and one prompt, the difference of the logit difference
f = z_target − z_competitor (the competitor fixed to ` the`, so that f is smooth) decomposes along the straight path between
their weights: f(B) − f(A) = ∫ ∇f(w_A + s·Δw)·Δw ds, estimated by the trapezoid ½(∇f(A) + ∇f(B))·Δw, coordinate by coordinate, where
Δw is the published difference. Everything is read: two published models, their gradients, their difference.

**Cell 09.** For the pairs (consecutive checkpoints, 240k to final), (base final, Instruct) and (240k, final), and the prompts
France, Spain, Japan and Egypt: f in each model; the attribution per tensor, per layer and sort, and its largest coordinates; the
completeness (the sum of attributions against the actual change); the concentration; and the exact change of every component's
contribution (embedding, attention and feed-forward per layer). The intermediate checkpoints use rotary base 10,000 and the final
model 100,000, a configuration difference that weights cannot carry, so completeness is expected to fail for pairs that cross it.

- **D1, high.** For base to Instruct on France (f changes by about 1.6), the trapezoid attributions sum to the actual change within 25 %.
- **D2, moderate.** The France margin is negative at every checkpoint of the trajectory, and the Spain margin changes sign at least once.
