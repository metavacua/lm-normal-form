## Why this batch

Two things were said in the session after batch 0015's results. The first: proof tools and proof languages exist, Datalog is a good grounding tool, and the structures of a model, of its code and of its files can be extracted into Datalog
forms by reflection, serialization and the like; so a statement such as "the generators I listed account for every continuous symmetry", which batch 0015 supported by a numerical rank on seven small models, should be derived from the structure
and not only observed. The second, about the KV cache: the residual stream changes under some of the symmetries and the cache under others, which "provides a measurable object or basis": is the residual stream fixed and invariant, or the
cache, or both, or neither?

A statement made in the session before this question (that K/V is "the more portable state across function-identical checkpoints") was too strong, and this batch corrects it: K/V is unchanged by the changes of the hidden basis (signed permutations,
rotations) and by the unit and norm gauges, and is moved by the attention-internal gauges (the value/output gauge, the key-plane scalars, the permutation of groups); the residual stream is moved by the first family and unchanged by the second.
Which is portable depends on which gauge two function-identical checkpoints differ by.

This batch has three parts, in the order of how much each can be trusted: (1) a program, in Datalog, that takes the dataflow of a forward pass (index spaces, tensors with their axes, and for each operation what it requires of the spaces it touches) and
derives, for every space, the group of changes of basis that act on it without changing what is computed; from that the dimension of the gauge group, and for any generator whether it is inside the group and what class of transformation each
state undergoes; (2) the same table measured on the weights, with batch 0015's generators, in float64; (3) the architecture of four real checkpoints read from their published metadata (configuration and safetensors header) and put to the program.
It is not a proof: see "What this does not do".

## What is built

- `ir.py` reifies an `Arch` (and three flags: epsilon zero or not, log-probabilities or logits, norm weights folded or not) into relations: **spaces** (the hidden coordinates `res`; per layer the head dimension of the queries and keys `qk`
  and of the values `ov`, with one independent copy per key/value group; the feed-forward units of the up branch `unit` and of the gate branch `ugate`; the groups `grp` and the heads inside a group `hin`; the outputs of the norm weights
  `na`, `nm`, `nf`; the positions and the vocabulary, on which no basis change acts), **tensors** (parameters and states, with the space of each axis), **operations** and the **roles** that they require of the spaces they touch (`lin`: it enters a linear map; `bilinear`: a pairing
  q . k; `rmsnorm`, `rmsnorm0`: the root mean square, with epsilon zero or not; `rope`; `nl`: an elementwise nonlinearity; `mono`: an elementwise product with a linear branch; `replica`: independent copies; `triv`; and the diagonal link of a norm weight). A tied head
  is a tensor that has the embedding's axis, so that the final norm weight maps the stream to the stream.
- `states.dl` (Souffle) holds the **library**, which is the mathematics that the program takes as given: a lattice of nine groups of changes of basis (trivial, permutations, signed permutations, a scalar times a signed permutation, orthogonal, a positive scalar
  times orthogonal, monomial, the commutant of the rotations of the planes, general linear) with their inclusions; the group that each role allows; the dimension of each group. Everything else is **derived**: the group of a space is the greatest lower bound of the groups allowed by the
  roles of the operations that touch it; the dimension is the sum over the spaces (with their copies) of the dimension of their groups, plus the shift of the logits by a vector of the hidden dimension when log-probabilities are observed and the head is untied; a **test generator** (a group
  element acting on the spaces of some kind: H1 a signed permutation of the hidden coordinates, H4 an orthogonal matrix on the stream with the norm weights folded, H4t the same with the weights kept and the readers conjugated, N1 scalings of the norm weights, M1 units permuted and scaled, A1 a general
  invertible matrix on the values, A3 complex scalars on the rotary planes, A6 permutations of groups and heads, and two that are not symmetries: A4 a general matrix on queries and keys, M2 a scale on the gate branch) is **allowed** if its group is inside the derived group of every space it acts on, and then **every state** is classified as unchanged
  or as undergoing a transformation of the class of the generator. The program also checks itself: every pair of groups has exactly one greatest lower bound, and every space gets exactly one group.
- `derive.py` runs Souffle (in its interpreter mode: nothing is compiled), `measure.py` applies the generators to weights and classifies how each traced state of the transformed model relates to the original's, `checkpoints.py` reads the metadata of four checkpoints, `grade19.py` grades.
- Souffle 2.5, the release package of its own project, with its published sha512 checked in the workflow.

Reading it as the question asked. The **residual stream** (`emb`, `h`, `hm`, `ao`, `mo`, `xhat_*`) is moved by the changes of the hidden basis (H1 by a signed permutation, H4 and H4t by a rotation) and is unchanged by every other generator. The **KV cache** (`k`, `v`) is unchanged by the changes of the hidden basis, by the norm gauge and by the unit gauge, and is moved
by the value/output gauge (the values, by a general invertible matrix), by the key-plane scalars (the keys, each rotary plane by a complex scalar) and by the permutation of groups. No single generator moves both; a composition of one from each family moves both. Neither is invariant under the whole group.
What is: the logits; the scores and patterns up to the order of the heads; the Gram matrix of the stream over positions (rotations preserve inner products). With a tied head and a learned final norm weight the program rejects the rotation of the stream (H4t is not allowed): the group of the stream drops to signed permutations, the program's
reading of claim H6c of batch 0015.

## Predictions, with severity

- **S1, low severity (the checks of the program).** In every derivation (the seven configurations, the toy models, the checkpoints, the real model) every pair of groups has exactly one greatest lower bound and every space has exactly one group.
- **S2, low severity (a reproduction).** The derived dimension of the gauge group of the seven configurations is 142, 148, 141, 121, 120, 82, 190, the rank deficiencies that batch 0015 measured.
- **S3, moderate severity (a reproduction).** On the four toy models every (generator, state) pair of every allowed generator agrees between the derived and the measured class (670 pairs); the rotation with the norm weights kept (H4t) is not allowed for the tied model; A4 and M2 are never allowed; the allowed generators change the logits by at most 1e-12 and the others by at least 1.
- **S4, low severity.** The dimension derived from the metadata is 455,040 for SmolLM2-135M-Instruct and for SmolLM2-135M and 700,672 for FloatLM 99M and for TriLM 99M; the reified architecture has exactly the parameter tensors of the file, with the same numbers of elements (134,515,008 and 99,762,688 parameters; 272 and 147 tensors).
- **S5, high severity (blind).** On the real weights of SmolLM2-135M-Instruct every (generator, state) pair of every allowed generator agrees between the derived and the measured class (151 pairs: H1, H4, N1, M1, A1, A3, A6 over the states of three layers; H4 acts on the folded, untied version of the model, as in batch 0015); the allowed generators change the logits by at most 1e-8; H4t (tied head), A4 and M2 are rejected by the derivation and change the logits by at least 1e-3.
- **S6, high severity (blind): the question.** In the measured table of the real model, the generators that move `h` are exactly H1 and H4 (and H4t if it were allowed), those that move `k` are exactly A3 and A6, those that move `v` are exactly A1 and A6, and no generator moves both `h` and `k` or `v`.
- **S7, moderate severity (blind).** For the real tied model the rotation of the stream with the norm weights kept (H4t) is rejected by the derivation, and applying it changes the logits by at least 1e-3.

## What was seen before registration

- Batch 0015's results (the measured rank deficiencies 142, 148, 141, 121, 120, 82, 190 of the seven configurations; the cache claims; the conformance of the formal forward pass to Transformers).
- The program was developed and run locally (Souffle's interpreter, numpy; no torch, no model): it reproduces the seven dimensions; its table for the toy models agrees with the measured table in all 670 pairs, after one fault of mine in the name of a class was corrected (the induced class of the rotation with the weights kept was spelled as a group instead of a class; the measurement had it right); the checks of the library are empty; the dimension and the conformance cells were run on the metadata of the four checkpoints (network access to the Hugging Face API, no weights), so S4 is a reproduction and S1 to S3 are as well.
- Two research agents' notes on the KV cache (read before this registration; none of it is used in the program): the gauge group of an attention layer is published (Wang and Wang for grouped-query attention with rotary embeddings; Tran et al. on completeness; a quantization paper states the rotary-plane scalar as an exact reparametrization and SmoothAttention uses its positive real part); the statement that the cache is unchanged by a change of the residual basis is, in the agents' reading, an unstated corollary of SliceGPT and QuaRot; llama.cpp (the pinned tag) rotates quantized caches by a Hadamard matrix; one codec paper reports that a learned orthogonal gauge lowers the KL of cache codecs by 44%; LARQL, whose framing is that a cache is derived state and an engine is a choice of canonical state with a contract, states the identity that K and V are functions of the stored residual, and (the agent's reading, not a statement of LARQL's) its fingerprint rule would refuse a checkpoint that differs by a hidden-basis gauge. Batch 0015 already had the cache claims K1 to K9, run in three arithmetics and on real weights.
- Not seen: any output of `measure.py` on trained weights.

## What this does not do

- It does not prove anything about transformers. Souffle derives the consequences of the library (the lattice of groups and the group that each role allows), which is mathematics taken as given here and tested only by the measurements. A wrong entry of the library would be seen only if a measured class disagreed.
- The generators are those of batch 0015. That the list is complete is supported by the dimension count (7 of 7 in batch 0015, and the same count derived here); it is not a theorem.
- Roles are assigned to the operations by me, in `ir.py`, by reading `model.py`; that the reified program is the program that runs is tested by the shapes (the traced forward pass equals `model.forward` to the last bit: asserted on the toy models, printed on the real one) and by the agreement of the tables, not by extraction from the code.
- One model, one random element per generator, one tolerance (1e-8) for the classes; the classification of a state as a class is a test with a threshold.
- Not here, and the reason for the next batches: statements for a proof assistant generated from the same facts (Lean over a generic transformer, for every nonlinearity), a rewrite-based proof by equality saturation (egglog, a Datalog with equality) of the block-level invariances, an exact-arithmetic certificate (dual numbers over a finite field) of the rank, the rules for LayerNorm models and for partial rotary embeddings with their dimensions predicted before they are measured, and a structure extracted from the runtimes' own code (ONNX graphs, candle, llama.cpp).
